## The Smallest Believable Pilot

**What it is:** A free 30-day readiness check on 10–30 real clinician NPIs from the employer's roster.
**What it produces:** A one-page readiness report per candidate — clear, incomplete, or flagged — with source cited.
**What it costs them:** Zero. A list of NPIs by email.
**What it costs you:** 30 minutes of your time.

This is the only pilot you should be running right now.

## What to Demo (10-minute walk-through)

### Step 1 — Homepage (60 seconds)
Open vitalcv.com. Show the NPI input.
Say: "This is all you need to start."

### Step 2 — Live NPI lookup (2 minutes)
Use a real NPI (suggest: ask employer to give you one of their recent candidates).
Show the passport loading: identity confirmed, exclusion checked, enrollment status.
Say: "That took 90 seconds. Here's what your team would otherwise spend 20 minutes confirming."

### Step 3 — Readiness status (2 minutes)
Point to the status: clear / incomplete / flagged.
Say: "This tells your team at a glance. Click in and you see exactly why — which source, what it found."

### Step 4 — Share link (1 minute)
Copy the passport link.
Say: "The clinician can share this with you directly. You don't need to do the lookup — they do it themselves before they even apply."

### Step 5 — The pilot ask (2 minutes)
Say: "I want to run your next 10 candidates through this before your next committee review. You send me the NPIs, I send you the report. Free. If it's useful, we talk."

**Stop there. Do not demo anything else.**

## What NOT to Promise

| Never say | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| "Replaces credentialing" | It doesn't |
| "Eliminates your process" | It doesn't |
| "Real-time PECOS data" | It's the public release, not live |
| "Verifies licenses" | NPPES is identity only — not licensure |
| "Compliant with [standard]" | Don't make compliance claims |
| "Blockchain-verified" | Not relevant, not helpful |
| "AI-powered" | Not the story — evidence-backed is the story |
| Any number you can't prove | "Saves 3 hours" only if you can document it |

**Only promise what you can deliver in 48 hours.**

## What Metrics Matter in the Pilot

### Primary (show these in your report)
- **Time to readiness signal**: how long did the check take
- **% candidates with clear status**: shows ROI immediately
- **% candidates with flags or gaps**: shows risk caught early
- **Sources checked per candidate**: NPPES / OIG / PECOS

### Secondary (collect for your own learning)
- How many candidates had issues they didn't know about
- Time employer spent reviewing the report
- Whether any flags matched actual onboarding delays

### What does NOT matter in pilot 1
- Proof format (JSON/ZIP/PDF)
- Audit trails
- Blockchain anything
- Integration with their ATS
- Dashboard UX
- Anything they didn't ask for

## Handling the Top 3 Objections

**"We already have a credentialing process."**
> "I'm not replacing it. I'm moving the discovery phase from week 2 to day zero. You still do your full process — you just start it with better information."

**"How is this different from a manual NPPES lookup?"**
> "We do NPPES, OIG, and PECOS simultaneously in 90 seconds, return a structured status, and the clinician can share it directly. Your team does manual lookups one at a time. At 30 candidates a month, that's real time."

**"We'd need to involve our compliance team."**
> "Of course. I'm not asking for system access or data sharing — just a list of NPIs you already have. The pilot produces a report you can share internally. No compliance exposure to start."
