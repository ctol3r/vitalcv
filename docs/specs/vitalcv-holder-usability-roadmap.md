# VitalCV Holder Usability Roadmap

## Lane A: Understanding + TTFV (Time-To-First-Value)
*   **Goal:** Clarify value immediately; eliminate vague processing.
*   **Target:** Identity resolution (NPI lookup) and readiness posture visibility on day 1.

## Lane B: CV Upload Confidence
*   **Goal:** Safe, legible, deterministic CV processing.
*   **Target:** Clear post-upload state showing parsed fields, missing fields, and immediate readiness improvements. Explain data retention and usage upfront.

## Lane C: Passport As Career Object
*   **Goal:** Living, portable career trust object.
*   **Target:** Brutalist, legible presentation of Identity, Checked, Missing, Blocked. No fake wallet metaphors.

## Lane D: Daily-Return Utility
*   **Goal:** Establish grounded reasons to open VitalCV daily.
*   **Target:** Track state board renewals, freshness/expiry monitoring, and readiness score recalculations tied to primary source rotation.

## Lane E: Apply / Share Continuity
*   **Goal:** Connect readiness naturally to hiring.
*   **Target:** Honest, comprehensible share language (e.g., "Preview link") bridging the credentialing-to-hiring gap.
