## Path 2 — Clinician Acquisition (longer to revenue, builds defensibility)

### The Loop
```
Clinician hears about VitalCV (word of mouth / SEO / community)
       ↓
Enters NPI → sees readiness snapshot → signs up
       ↓
Shares passport with employer or staffing agency
       ↓
Employer sees the value → comes inbound
       ↓
Employer pays for readiness screening at scale
```

### Acquisition channels (in priority order)

**1. Nursing / healthcare communities (free, immediate)**
- Post in r/nursing, r/medicine, r/physicianassistant
- Frame: "Built a tool that checks your credential readiness in 30 seconds — would love feedback from real nurses/PAs"
- Do NOT pitch — invite genuine feedback
- Target: 50 signups in first 2 weeks

**2. Twitter/X healthcare threads (free, compounding)**
- Tweet the demo path: video of entering NPI → seeing readiness in 30s
- Tag relevant healthcare / credentialing accounts
- Target: 200 impressions per tweet, 5–10 signups per post

**3. LinkedIn founder post (free, immediate)**
- Write an honest founder story: "I'm building a tool to help clinicians stop re-credentialing from scratch. Here's what I found."
- Include the demo screenshot
- Target: 500 views, 20–30 signups

**4. Direct DM to travel nurses (free, targeted)**
- Travel nurses re-credential constantly — highest pain point
- Find on Reddit, Facebook travel nurse groups, Instagram
- DM with: "I built something that checks your NPI readiness in 30 seconds — would you try it?"
- Target: 10 conversations, 5 signups

**5. SEO (free, slow)**
- Optimize for: "NPI lookup", "credential readiness check", "OIG exclusion check"
- Already have the route structure for this
- Returns take 60–90 days — start now, don't wait on it
