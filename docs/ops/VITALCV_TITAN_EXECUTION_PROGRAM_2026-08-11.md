## 1. Founder-locked product and visual thesis

The product loop is one transaction, not a collection of portals:

```text
CV Wallet
  -> source-aware readiness
  -> explainable opportunity
  -> clinician-selected ApplicationPacket
  -> employer review of that exact packet
  -> accepted head start and Recognition
  -> actual start lineage
  -> consented reuse in the next career move
```

The 2026-08-13 founder decision extends the live Direction D homepage register;
it does not retire Direction D or change shared-header geometry.

- **Documentary clinical photography** supplies humanity, ambition, place, and
  stakes. It shows clinicians acting, preparing, collaborating, transitioning,
  or working in quiet clinical environments.
- **Proprietary tactile artifacts** make the system memorable: the CV Wallet,
  evidence tiles, consent gate, ApplicationPacket, employer review,
  Recognition, and reuse appear as physical, inspectable career objects.
- **Warm paper, ink, restrained source green, Fraunces, and calm purposeful
  motion** form the route register that WO-12 must reconcile with EC-20 through
  a dated amendment before implementation.
- **Opportunity is visible immediately.** A clinician should see that VitalCV
  can help move a career forward, not encounter a wall of credentialing copy.

Do not use dense network galaxies, generic SaaS card wallpaper, fake
dashboards, staged white-coat photography, invented metrics, unsupported
sources, decorative success color, or product theatre disconnected from real
state. The founder-supplied clinical and graph images are art-direction
references unless production rights are confirmed.

Photography used in production must have a recorded license, consent and asset
provenance, contain no visible patient identifiers or PHI, and remain usable at
the intended crop. Tactile artwork must be owned by VitalCV or licensed for the
production use; its state labels remain truthful and self-identifying.

## 3. Program rules

1. One work order equals one branch, one independently reversible PR,
   intentional commits, and one ledger entry in that same PR.
2. Claim-check open and merged PRs plus remote intent branches before coding.
   Preserve user-owned dirty work; cut a clean worktree from current
   `origin/main`.
3. An implementation is not complete because code exists, `main` moved, or a
   URL returned HTTP 200. Required checks must be green, the merge state must be
   `CLEAN`, Railway must report the exact merge SHA, and the changed flow must be
   exercised on production.
4. Security, privacy, authorization, consent, source truth, packet immutability,
   auditability, and anti-enumeration remain binding. Visual ambition cannot
   soften them.
5. No numerical outcome claim becomes public until its cohort, baseline,
   period, sample size, data lineage, and qualification exist.
6. NPI identifies a provider; it never authorizes an employer or organization.
   Matching never becomes a hidden employer ranking or automatic rejection.
7. Reuse requires fresh clinician consent. Previous recipients inherit no
   access; stale, contradicted, unavailable, revoked, and superseded evidence
   remain explicit.
8. Shared public chrome, security-enforcement flips, destructive migrations,
   unsupported source integrations, and high-stakes automated employment
   decisions require separately named authority.

## 6. Wave 2 — Find Work and MATCHA acquisition

### WO-13 · Public opportunity field

Transform `/explore` from a dashboard/feed into an editorial opportunity field
with real roles, clinical-setting imagery, generous hierarchy, and filters for
specialty, profession, location, schedule, and employment type.

Every rendered listing must expose server-derived provenance:

- source name and source URL;
- observed time;
- availability state and confidence;
- application mode;
- compensation and compensation provenance when the source supplies it.

External roles say **View original listing**. Only integrated opportunities may
say **Apply with VitalCV**. Missing compensation, stale feeds, closed or expired
roles, and unavailable source pages stay explicit. There is no public
readiness score or automatic eligibility verdict.

### WO-13B · Opportunity discovery controls

Reach HiringCafe-class discovery utility without cloning its visual identity or
creating a second matcher. Add a horizontal opportunity-lens rail for fresh
source observations, supplied compensation, integrated application, locums,
remote care, and profession paths. Extend the advanced public contract with:

- source-observation window;
- original-listing versus Apply with VitalCV path;
- supplied, limited, and missing compensation or benefits detail;
- recent update, title, and organization sorting;
- normalized, shareable URL state.

Lens cards remain ordinary links without JavaScript; carousel controls are an
enhancement. Filters read only explicit canonical opportunity truth. They never
infer pay, benefits, fit, readiness, eligibility, employer preference, or
sensitive facts. Natural-language intent and personal explanation remain in
signed-in MATCHA; no public control may auto-apply or auto-reject.

### WO-14 · Opportunity detail and signed-in MATCHA

Public detail explains the role, organization, setting, schedule, supplied
compensation, source, observation time, availability, and application path.
Signed-in MATCHA adds **Why this may fit**, evidence gaps, uncertainty, and
clinician-controlled next steps.

Preserve one continuity chain:

```text
Recommendation
  -> opportunity version
  -> match explanation
  -> disclosure composer
  -> sealed ApplicationPacket
```

Matching stays explainable and clinician-side. Employers receive no hidden
ranking, inferred sensitive data, or automatic rejection. Changed or closed
opportunities remain visible as changed or closed rather than disappearing into
a misleading recommendation state.

## 8. Wave 4 — clinician product transaction

1. Finish the authorized `ApplicationPacket` reader with clinician ownership,
   active employer organization membership, explicit administrator authority,
   packet-version selection, hash validation, legacy behavior, and
   anti-enumeration. Use real-PostgreSQL positive, negative, cross-application,
   and tamper tests.
2. Render submitted evidence separately from current Wallet evidence. Added,
   changed, resolved, stale, unavailable, contradicted, removed, and revoked
   states are explicit; historical values are never recomputed.
3. Build one selective-disclosure composer whose preview and sealed packet use
   the same canonical input. Required and optional evidence, omissions,
   recipient, purpose, consent, and receipt remain inspectable.
4. Consolidate the clinician experience into CV Wallet, MATCHA, and the current
   application rather than adding portals.
5. Express Start Agent and Hiring Agent as bounded next-action assistance. AI
   may draft; it may not silently mutate evidence, certify facts, decide
   readiness, rank candidates for employers, send a packet, or apply for a role.

## 9. Wave 5 — employer acceptance, Recognition, start, and reuse

1. Converge employer application list/detail and every decision command on the
   canonical organization-authorized service.
2. Record clarification, accept-as-head-start, and do-not-proceed atomically
   with actor, organization, exact packet version, Decision Capsule, audit
   event, durable notification intent, and idempotency.
3. Tie Recognition to the exact packet and decision. Recognition means that an
   employer accepted that packet as a head start; it is not credentialing,
   privileging, or an employment guarantee.
4. Keep offer acceptance, credentialing start, intended start, and actual start
   as separate events with correction and revocation lineage.
5. Reuse earlier evidence only after fresh clinician consent. Stale or revoked
   evidence remains visible, and a previous recipient receives no inherited
   access.

## 11. Wave 7 — quality and release system

Every visual PR includes:

- before/after evidence at 390px, 768px, 1440px, and 1728px;
- desktop and mobile motion recordings when motion changes;
- reduced-motion, no-JavaScript, 200% zoom, and both supported-theme frames;
- computed typography, contrast, overflow, focus, and keyboard evidence;
- a documented duplicate-intent search and one named creative owner;
- a review-environment URL and the completed visual scorecard;
- production-build Playwright, accessibility, route, copy, claims, design, and
  visual-regression gates;
- a practical LCP-image target of at most 250 KB and motion-payload target of at
  most 1.5 MB, with no required WebGL;
- controlled-profile targets of LCP below 2.5 seconds, CLS below 0.1, and INP
  below 200 milliseconds, recording the profile and measured result rather than
  asserting an unmeasured pass.

Every work order runs typecheck, build, affected tests, and the repository's
aggregate test command. Authorization, packet, organization, or durable-state
changes also run the real-PostgreSQL harness and negative cases. Security-
relevant standards changes require their applicable conformance and misuse
tests.

After all required head-SHA checks are green and GitHub reports `CLEAN`, use a
squash or rebase merge. Then verify Railway's exact `/api/version` SHA, service
health, relevant cache policy, and the changed production flow on desktop and

## 12. Wave 8 — pilot and defensibility

Initial concentration:

- agency-assisted physicians first;
- locums as the highest-frequency subsegment;
- advanced practice providers second;
- job listings as acquisition and workflow inputs, not the moat.

Pilot acceptance:

- 10 real clinicians and 2 real employers participate with consent;
- every submitted packet receives an employer response;
- at least 3 packets are explicitly accepted as a head start;
- measure employer requests avoided, clarification count,
  packet-open-to-decision time, and actual-start lineage;
- if employer requests avoided remains zero, treat that as evidence about the
  thesis rather than manufacturing a positive narrative;
- publish no outcome claim until cohort, baseline, period, sample size, data
  lineage, and qualification are recorded.

Instrument durable events for NPI start/completion, opportunity view, MATCHA
explanation, disclosure preview, consent, submission, packet open,
clarification, acceptance, Recognition, intended start, actual start, and reuse.
Each analytic event must map to a real state transition and pass privacy review.
