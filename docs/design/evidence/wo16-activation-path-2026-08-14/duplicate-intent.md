## Benchmark translation

- HiringCafe's low-friction path to visible work informed making an opportunity
  the destination of onboarding rather than ending at a credential record.
  The opportunity itself remains owned by the canonical `/explore` work.
- Medallion informed the value of a visible, inspectable workflow instead of a
  long buyer explanation.
- Abridge and Palantir informed the confidence and pacing of one high-level
  explanatory scene rather than another grid of generic SaaS cards.

The implementation translates those patterns into VitalCV's differentiator:
start from an NPI, preserve source states, keep the record clinician-controlled,
reach source-labelled work, and record the exact employer response. It does
not copy competitor layouts, fabricate a result, create another opportunity
board, or create another animation engine.
