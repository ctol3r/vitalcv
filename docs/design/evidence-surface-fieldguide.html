T1 · T2 · T6 design-language

The atom is a chip with a memory.

Status is never a color alone, an icon alone, or a bare word. It's a dot, a word, and a mono provenance line — source and timestamp — underneath. Six states, each with its own register. Red belongs to revoked and nothing else.

✗ reject anti-pattern

What the research says we must not become.

Twenty anti-patterns surfaced across the 100 sources. The load-bearing few, kept visible so no wave quietly reintroduces them:

✗ 01
The bare status word
"Verified" with no source, no date. Never a status without provenance.
✗ 02
The single fit score
"95% match," interview odds, salary guesses. Reason codes or nothing.
✗ 03
The dead artifact
Buy-once PDF résumés that never decay or revoke. Ours stays alive.
✗ 04
Badge soup
Walls of compliance logos in place of a dated control + audit event.
✗ 05
Manufactured urgency
Fake countdowns, streak-loss, "no time to think" consent. Deliberate wins.
✗ 06
Edit buttons on audit rows
Corrections are appended, never overwritten. Say so in the microcopy.
✗ 07
Fuzzy identity matching
An NPI ranked "close enough" is a truth violation. Exact-match only.
✗ 08
Crypto bragging
TPS numbers, chain hype. The chain is the engine, never the headline.
