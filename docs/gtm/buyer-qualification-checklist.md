# Buyer Qualification Checklist

Use this checklist during discovery calls to determine if an organization is a fit for the 30-Day PSV Readiness Pilot. 

## Ideal Buyer Roles
* VP / Director of Talent Acquisition
* Director of Medical Staff Services / MSO Leader
* Credentialing Manager / Director
* Provider Enrollment Leader
* VP / Director of Staffing Operations
* Locums Operations Leader
* Medical Group COO / VP Operations

## Discovery Questions
1. **The Baseline:** "When a new clinician signs an offer or enters your pipeline, how many days does their file sit before your credentialing team does the first primary source check?"
2. **The Pain:** "How often do start dates slip because a missing license or an OIG flag is discovered in week 3 instead of week 1?"
3. **The Workflow:** "Are you currently managing this process in spreadsheets, an ATS (like Greenhouse), or dedicated credentialing software (like CredentialStream)?"
4. **The Volume:** "Roughly how many clinicians do you onboard or re-credential per month?"
5. **The Blockers:** "If we could hand your team a source-backed packet on day zero showing NPPES identity, OIG LEIE federal exclusion posture, and PECOS public enrollment posture — with explicit limitation notes for everything that still needs PSV — how would that change your workflow?"

## Disqualifying Buyer Signs (Do Not Pitch Pilot)
* **Wants Full Automation:** They expect VitalCV to replace their credentialing committee and make the final privileging decision.
* **Requires Immediate Legacy IT Integration:** They cannot do a pilot without deep, on-premise integration with their 15-year-old HRIS system.
* **Volume is Too Low:** They only onboard 1-2 clinicians a quarter (the pain isn't acute enough).
* **Cannot Share Data:** They are unwilling or unable to provide 10-30 NPIs for a simple simulated pilot.

## Scoring Criteria

### Urgency Score (1-5)
* 5: Start dates are slipping weekly; active loss of revenue/placements.
* 3: Process is slow and annoying, but they manage.
* 1: "We have a 90-day onboarding window and we just wait."

### Data Readiness Score (1-5)
* 5: Can provide 30 NPIs tomorrow; knows their current baseline time-to-first-check.
* 3: Can provide NPIs, but has no idea what their baseline is.
* 1: Requires 4 weeks of legal review just to share public NPIs.

### Pilot Feasibility Score (1-5)
* 5: Understands this is a day-zero "head start" tool, not a full CVO replacement.
* 3: Wants some custom state boards that aren't live yet, but willing to test against OIG LEIE federal exclusions and PECOS public enrollment posture.
* 1: Demands real-time CAQH and NPDB access immediately.

## Next Action Mapping
* **Total Score 12-15:** Immediate Pilot Fit. Send post-demo email and request NPIs.
* **Total Score 8-11:** Nurture. They need the tool, but might need education on why day-zero checks matter more than full-stack automation.
* **Total Score < 8:** Disqualify. Not a fit for the current VitalCV wedge.
