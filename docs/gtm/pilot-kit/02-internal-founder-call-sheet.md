# VitalCV — Internal Founder Call Sheet
## Chris Toler · Internal Use Only · April 2026

---

## Before Every Call

**Confirm you know:**
- [ ] Their role (credentialing director / VP staffing ops / CMO / practice admin)
- [ ] Approx. clinician volume per month (check LinkedIn, website, or prior outreach notes)
- [ ] State they primarily operate in (to confirm if your state-board lane covers them)
- [ ] Whether they have an existing CVO or credentialing software

**Mindset:** You are not selling software. You are offering to measure something they likely do not know how to measure. If they feel heard, they book the pilot.

---

## Call Structure (20–30 minutes)

### Opening (0–2 min)
> "Hi [Name], thanks for making time. Quick agenda: I'll take about 60 seconds to tell you what we do, then I want to spend most of this call understanding how credentialing works on your end today — fair?"

*This signals you are not going to pitch for 30 minutes. It earns real conversation.*

---

### Discovery (2–15 min)

Ask these in order. Stop at the first one that opens a real thread and follow it.

1. **The baseline question (always ask this):**
   > "When a clinician signs an offer letter with you, what happens to their application in the first 48 hours? Who touches it, and what are they waiting on?"

2. **The pain surface question:**
   > "Has your team ever found out a clinician had an OIG flag or an expired license after you already had them on the schedule? How does that usually get caught?"

3. **The velocity question:**
   > "If I asked you right now — how many days does it typically take from offer to start — would you know that number, or would it take some digging?"
   
   *If they say "we track it" → ask what it is and write it down — this is your baseline.*
   *If they say "we don't track it" → that is your pilot pitch. You will measure it for them.*

4. **The resource question:**
   > "How many coordinators are spending time on that initial verification window — the period before the file reaches committee?"

5. **The stakes question (if needed to surface urgency):**
   > "When a start date slips by a week or two, what's the downstream effect — does it get absorbed, or does it cost you real placement revenue?"

---

### Pitch (15–20 min) — Only After Discovery

> "Here's what we do: when a clinician applies, your team drops in their NPI. We pull NPPES identity, OIG federal exclusions against our latest source-release check, and PECOS public enrollment status. If you have a configured state-board lane, we run that too. We package all of that into a proof pack — a deterministic artifact your team can act on before the file even hits a coordinator's queue.
>
> We don't replace your credentialing committee. We don't replace your CVO. We don't claim to check NPDB, DEA, or ABMS because we don't. What we do is eliminate the first 10 to 30 days of 'we don't know yet.'"

**Then go quiet.** Let them react.

---

### The Pilot Ask (20–25 min)

> "I'm running a 30-day structured pilot with a small number of credentialing teams right now. It's no charge during the pilot window. You give me 10 to 30 NPIs — no other PII, no IT involvement — and I'll run them through and show you the time-risk delta against your own baseline. At the end we do a 30-minute review call together. If it doesn't move the needle, you've lost an hour. If it does, we talk about what comes next."

**Close:** > "Does that sound like something worth running?"

*If yes → move to scope confirmation (below).*
*If hesitant → see objection handling card.*

---

### Scope Confirmation (25–30 min — only if they say yes)

Capture these four items before ending the call:

| Item | What to ask |
|---|---|
| **NPI roster** | "Can you pull together 10–30 NPIs? These can be pending applications or recent hires." |
| **State** | "What state(s) do most of your clinicians practice in? I need to confirm our licensure lane covers you." |
| **Baseline** | "Do you have a rough sense of your current time-to-start, or should we establish that as part of the pilot?" |
| **Review operator** | "Who on your team would be the main person reviewing the results — is that you, or someone else I should loop in?" |

Send the scope doc within 24 hours of this call. No measurement starts until it's signed.

## Qualification Gates

**Green light — proceed:**
- Onboards 5+ clinicians per month
- Has a coordinator or director who touches credential files before committee
- Will share NPIs and join the wrap-up call

**Yellow — probe further:**
- Only onboards 2–4/month (pilot math still works, but ROI story is thinner)
- Uses an outsourced CVO exclusively (may need to position as CVO accelerator)
- Says "our process is fine" — ask what fine means in days

**Red — do not proceed:**
- Wants VitalCV to make the final privileging decision
- Requires full HRIS integration before running a pilot
- Onboards fewer than 2 clinicians per quarter
- Expects NPDB, DEA, or ABMS coverage in the pilot window

## What You Must Never Say on a Call

| ❌ Do not say | ✅ Say instead |
|---|---|
| "Real-time OIG check" | "OIG exclusion check against our latest available source release" |
| "We validate their license" | "We confirm NPI identity from NPPES — licensure is a separate state-board lane" |
| "Blockchain" or "crypto" | "Cryptographically signed audit trail" |
| "Hire instantly" | "Start clinicians in days, not months" |
| "HIPAA certified" | "HIPAA-aligned" |
| "SOC 2 certified" | Nothing — don't mention it |
| "All 50 states" | Name only the specific state you have a lane for |
| "We replace your CVO" | Never |
| Any specific ROI dollar figure | "We'll measure against your own baseline" |
