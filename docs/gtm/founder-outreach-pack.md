## Objection Handling

### 9. “Send me more info”
**Reply:** "Happy to. The easiest way to see if this works for you is to look at our 30-Day Readiness Pilot scope: [Link to /pilot]. It breaks down exactly what we check (Identity, OIG, PECOS) and what we don't. Take a look, and let me know if you want to run a few NPIs through it."

### 10. “We already have credentialing software (or a CVO)”
**Reply:** "That’s perfect. We don't replace your credentialing software or your CVO. VitalCV sits at the very top of the funnel. We give your team an instant, verified packet on day zero so they aren't waiting weeks to find out a candidate has an OIG flag. It makes your existing software and team faster."

### 11. “Is this replacing our credentialing committee?”
**Reply:** "Absolutely not. VitalCV provides a verified head start, not a final credentialing decision. We gather the primary source data, package it in an immutable audit envelope, and hand it to your team so they can make the final decision without the manual grunt work."

### 12. “Is this blockchain/crypto?”
**Reply:** "No. We use standard, verifiable digital signatures (like the technology that secures your web browser) to ensure the data we pull hasn't been tampered with. It's an immutable audit log, not a cryptocurrency or public blockchain."

### 13. “What do you need from us to start the pilot?”
**Reply:** "Just a roster of 10 to 30 clinician NPIs and the name of the person on your team who will review the results. No IT integration, no PHI, and no disruption to your current workflow."
