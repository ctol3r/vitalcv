D1

Invent the Graph

define the substrate

A Career Evidence Graph is a single, shared, append-only network in which every clinician, organization, source, credential, and piece of evidence is a node, and every relationship between them is a tiered, source-bound edge. Where a résumé is a private document a clinician re-writes, and a credentialing file is an institution's private copy, the graph is one connected structure that all of them read from and none of them owns alone. It exists so that a fact verified once — a license read from a state board, an employment confirmed by a health system — never has to be verified again by anyone else who relies on the same node.

The three primitives · everything in the graph is one of these

Node — an entity

A durable thing the network refers to: a person, an org, an authority, a credential, a record. Nodes are nouns that persist — they are referenced by many edges and outlive any single relationship.

Edge — an assertion

A directed, typed claim from one node to another — holds, issued_by, employed_at. Each edge carries its own tier, observed_at, and receipt. The edge is an EvidenceObject; the graph is the index over them.

Anchor — a shared node

Any node two or more clinicians both connect to: a board, an employer, a specialty. Anchors are where trust becomes transitive and verification cost is amortized across everyone attached to them.

Career Evidence Graph architecture · five planes, bottom-up
Architecture · capture once at the bottom, reuse at every plane aboveappend-only · source-bound · clinician-owned
Plane 05Network
The compounding asset. Shared anchors, corroboration clusters, and transitive paths. Every new clinician or source attached to an existing anchor strengthens the whole graph for everyone already on it.
Plane 04Trust
The derived plane. Six trust dimensions and readiness states, computed by propagating tier and corroboration along edges. Nothing here is stored as a fact — it is recomputed from the planes below (see D4).
Plane 03Relationship
The connective plane. Twenty-four edge types binding nodes — holds issued_by corroborates supersedes. Edges are evidence-bearing and directional; the topology lives here (see D3).
Plane 02Node
The entity plane. One hundred node types across twelve families — clinicians, organizations, authorities, credentials, evidence, taxonomy. Each node is content-addressed and referenced, never duplicated (see D2).
Plane 01Evidence
The atomic plane (W215). The EvidenceObject — a single source-attributed, immutable assertion. The graph is an index over these. Every node and edge dereferences to evidence; nothing is asserted without it.
What the graph creates · trust, reputation, portability
Creates trustprovenance × corroboration

Trust is created where an edge traces to an independent authority and is confirmed by more than one source. A license edge to the state board is trusted; the same license corroborated by two employers is trusted more. Trust is a property of the path, not the claim.

Creates reputationaccumulation over time

Reputation is created where evidence accumulates and connects — a rising arc of leadership edges, a growing body of authored publications, a deepening cluster of recognitions. Reputation is the shape of a clinician's subgraph read across a career, not any single node.

Creates portabilityownership + receipt

Portability is created where a node is clinician-owned and carries a signed receipt. Because the destination trusts the source node directly — not VitalCV — the clinician can present the edge anywhere and the receiver verifies it without re-collecting. Trust travels with the person.

Architecture answer · what is a Career Evidence GraphIt is professional trust, made into a shared structure. Nodes are the entities a career touches; edges are the source-bound assertions that connect them; anchors are the shared nodes where trust becomes transitive. Build it once, append forever, and the cost of verification falls to zero for the second person who relies on any node.

Most trustauthorities & credentials

Authorities mint it; credentials carry it. A node's trust ceiling is set by the authority at the end of its provenance path. The state board, ABMS, NPPES, and NPDB are the highest-weight nodes in the graph — and the license, board cert, and privilege nodes that descend from them inherit that weight.

Most network effectanchors

Shared nodes are the network. Organizations, authorities, and specialties are reused by thousands of clinicians, so every verification they back is amortized across all of them. The 1,000th clinician at a health system inherits an anchor the 1st paid to establish.

Most compoundingscholarship & provenance

Additive nodes only grow. Publications, leadership, recognition, and — above all — corroboration and verification events never decay. Each new one adds permanent weight, so a career's subgraph is strictly more valuable every year it is maintained.

D3

Relationship System

connect the nodes

Relationships are the graph's load-bearing layer — twenty-four directed, typed edges, each one an EvidenceObject in its own right. An edge is never a bare pointer: it carries the tier, observed_at, source, and receipt of the assertion it makes. Read an edge as a sentence — Clinician → HOLDS → License — and the three nodes it touches plus the provenance it carries are the whole claim. Edges fall into three behaviors that decide how the graph ages: permanent, expiring, and trust-bearing.

D4

Trust Flow Model

propagate trust through the graph

Trust is not stored on a clinician — it is propagated to them, along edges, from the authorities that mint it. Every clinician's standing is recomputed by flowing tier and corroboration inward from source nodes, aggregating into six trust dimensions, and resolving outward into readiness and network value. Because the inputs are immutable and the path is auditable, the output is never a black-box score — it is a traceable sum of source-bound evidence.

Read left to right · authorities mint trust → it accumulates into six dimensions → which resolve into portable readiness. Network Trust feeds back: a well-corroborated clinician strengthens every anchor they touch.

The seven laws of propagation
01
Trust originates only at authorities.

No node asserts its own trust. Trust enters the graph at Source nodes — a board, a registry, a primary-source verifier — and flows outward. A clinician's standing is always borrowed from the authorities they trace to.

02
Trust flows along edges, weighted by tier.

Each edge transmits trust scaled by its tier — T4 signed primary-source passes nearly all of it; T1 self-report passes almost none. Trust attenuates with every hop away from its source.

03
Independent corroboration multiplies, not adds.

Two sources that agree are worth more than twice one. A corroborates edge from a genuinely independent source raises confidence super-linearly; a dependent confirmation adds nothing. Independence is the scarce input.

04
Trust accumulates into dimensions, not a single score.

Evidence routes to one of six dimensions by class. A clinician is never "trusted" in the abstract — they are credentialing-ready, research-strong, leadership-proven. Context picks which dimension matters.

05
Volatile trust decays; immutable trust does not.

Trust on expiring edges fades on the claim's clock and must be refreshed by a new observation. Trust on permanent edges never decays — it converts to historical context but keeps its weight.

06
Trust crosses shared anchors — it is transitive.

When two clinicians share a verified anchor, trust the network paid to establish on one is available to the other. This is the only law that moves trust between clinicians, and it is what makes the graph a network.

07
Every propagation is auditable.

The output carries its inputs. Any trust value can be expanded to the exact edges, sources, and receipts that produced it. There is no opaque score — only a traceable sum of source-bound evidence.

How trust compounds · three engines, over a career
Engine A

Accumulation

More evidence, monotonic. Each new publication, role, and recognition is a permanent node. The body of work only grows — a 20-year career's subgraph is strictly denser than a 5-year one.

Engine B

Corroboration density

Same facts, more confirmations. Every re-verification and independent attestation thickens existing edges. The same license, confirmed by three more sources over a decade, is worth far more than the day it was first read.

Engine C

Network reuse

Shared anchors, amortized. As more clinicians attach to the same boards, employers, and specialties, each anchor's verification cost spreads thinner and its trust value rises for everyone on it. Value per node climbs with graph size.

Deliverable · Trust Propagation FrameworkTrust flows in from authorities, accumulates into six dimensions, decays only where the world changes, and crosses shared anchors to reach other clinicians. It compounds on three engines — accumulation, corroboration density, and network reuse — so a maintained career is strictly more trustworthy every year, and a larger graph is strictly more valuable to everyone in it.
D5

Graph Experiences

one graph, four readers

The same graph answers four different questions depending on who is looking — so the experience is one substrate rendered through four lenses, never four separate products. Each reader enters at a different node, traverses different edges, and reads trust in the dimension that matters to them. What none of them does is re-collect: every lens reads from evidence that was captured once.

Persona 01

Clinician

"This is my career — and I carry it." Owns the subgraph, fills its gaps, presents it.
What they see

Their own subgraph as a living career — every node they own, every edge's tier, and the gaps before anyone else notices them. The graph is autobiography with receipts.

How they navigate trust

Walk outward from themselves to sources; see which edges are signed, stale, or unbound; refresh a volatile license or request an attestation to lift a dimension.

Recruiter

"Is this person a fit, fast?" Triages many subgraphs against one role.
What they see

A readiness-first collapse of each candidate's subgraph — specialty anchor, trajectory shape, mobility state — scannable in seconds across a whole pipeline.

How they navigate trust

Enter at a Specialty or Organization anchor and traverse to clinicians attached to it. Reads dimension badges, not raw evidence; expands only where a role demands it.

How they navigate trust

Read the dimension that matches the role — Clinical for a hospitalist, Research for a PI — then drill into corroboration density and NPDB / malpractice standing for risk.

Architecture answer · one substrate, four entry pointsThe clinician enters at themselves, the recruiter at an anchor, the credentialer at a credential, the employer at a role. Same nodes, same edges, same evidence — only the traversal and the dimension in focus change. No reader re-collects what another already captured; the graph turns four separate workflows into four readings of one record.

Network Synthesis

the six answers

The five deliverables are one substrate. Read together they answer the wave's six questions and describe how the Career Evidence Graph turns VitalCV from a credential-readiness product into a Provider Career Evidence Network.

Q1What is the Career Evidence Graph?
A single shared, append-only network where every clinician, organization, authority, credential, and piece of evidence is a node, and every relationship is a tiered, source-bound edge. It is professional trust made into a structure — so a fact verified once is never re-verified by anyone who relies on the same node.
Q2What are its nodes?
One hundred first-class types across twelve families — actors, organizations, authorities, credentials, education, employment, scholarship, leadership, recognition, taxonomy, provenance, and derived. Authorities mint trust, credentials carry it, anchors share it, scholarship compounds it.
Q3What are its relationships?
Twenty-four directed, evidence-bearing edges in four behaviors — permanent (earned, authored, led), expiring (holds, privileged_at), trust-bearing (verified_by, corroborates), and structural (part_of, specializes_in). corroborates is the edge that turns records into a network.
Q4How does trust propagate?
It originates only at authorities, flows along edges weighted by tier, attenuates with distance, multiplies under independent corroboration, accumulates into six dimensions, decays only on volatile claims, and crosses shared anchors to reach other clinicians — every step auditable back to source.
Q5How does trust compound?
On three engines: accumulation (permanent nodes only grow), corroboration density (the same facts confirmed by more independent sources), and network reuse (shared anchors amortized across more clinicians). A maintained career is strictly more trustworthy every year.
Q6How does this become a network?
The moment a node is shared. Verify a board once and trust every certification it issues; confirm an employer once and anchor every clinician who worked there. Each new clinician and source strengthens the anchors everyone already depends on — and the network, not any single record, becomes the compounding asset.
DeterminationThe graph is not a database — it is the memory of a profession. Nodes are what a career touches, edges are the source-bound assertions that connect them, and trust is the value that flows across both. Build the shared anchors right and every clinician, source, and verification makes the whole network stronger. This is the substrate the Provider Career Evidence Network grows out of.
