VitalCV / Foundation Layer / W215 · Career Evidence

Today VitalCV verifies a credential. The foundation makes the verification a byproduct of remembering a career.

This is not the next feature. It is the substrate the next decade of products grows out of. One object — the EvidenceObject — captured once and owned by the clinician, accumulates into Professional Memory, renders as a Career Timeline, resolves into Mobility Readiness, and connects into a Provider Career Evidence Network. We do not optimize for today's credentialing workflow. We design the system every future product emerges from.

D1

The Evidence Object

invent the atomic unit

An EvidenceObject is a single, source-attributed assertion about a clinician, permanently bound to where it came from and the moment it was observed. It is not a document, not a credential, and not a profile field. It is an assertion plus its provenance, captured once and never mutated. "Dr. Reyes held an active California medical license — observed at the Medical Board of California on 2026-03-01." Everything else in the foundation is a view over a collection of these.

Trust model · what makes evidence trustworthy
01 · Provenancewhere

Trust is a property of origin, not formatting. A claim read from the issuing authority outranks one attested by an employer, which outranks self-report. The channel is recorded on the object and is non-negotiable.

02 · Independencecorroboration

One source asserts; independent sources corroborate. A license confirmed by the board and reflected in two employers' attestations is stronger than any single read. Corroboration is an edge in the graph (D5).

03 · Recencyfreshness

Freshness is measured against claim volatility, not the calendar. A 2009 degree is never stale. A license observed 14 months ago is. Each claim type carries its own decay clock.

04 · Immutabilityintegrity

Objects are content-addressed and never edited. The id is the proof of integrity. Correction means a new object linked by lineage — the original observation is preserved as history.

05 · Ownershipstanding

Evidence is bound to the clinician, who holds standing to present it. Delegation is explicit and revocable; unbound evidence cannot be presented as the subject's own.

06 · Tieringlegibility

The five properties resolve to a single legible tier T1–T4 — the one number a reviewer reads in a second. The tier never claims more certainty than the source provides.

Reusability & compounding — the two questions that matter What makes evidence reusable: it is decoupled from any one form. Captured once, a signed license object serves credentialing, hiring, privileging, and locums without re-asking the source — write once, present many. What compounds: longitudinal evidence (tenure, publication count, leadership progression) and re-verification density — every maintenance pass and every corroborating source makes the same object more trustworthy over time. What expires: only volatile claims (license, DEA, board cycle, malpractice, privileges); immutable facts never expire — they become historical context.
D2

Professional Memory

invent the durable record

Professional Memory is the durable, append-only ledger of a clinician's EvidenceObjects across all eleven classes — the system remembering a career rather than re-deriving it on every application. A résumé is a claim the clinician re-writes each time. Professional Memory is the opposite: a record they accumulate once, that institutions read from. It answers four questions about what a career leaves behind.

Architecture answerProfessional Memory is one ledger, four destinies. Every EvidenceObject lands in the same append-only record, but its class decides whether it stays identity, settles into history, builds reputation, or feeds trust. The clinician never re-enters it; the institution never re-derives it. Memory is the asset that makes every downstream layer cheap.
D3

Career Timeline Experience

invent the way a career is read

The Career Timeline is how Professional Memory becomes legible — one substrate, three lenses. A clinician experiences it as a narrative they own; a recruiter scans it for fit; an employer audits it for risk. Same objects, same timeline, different altitude. The timeline is the first product a clinician actually holds, and the surface every evaluator already trusts.

Three readers · one substrate
Cliniciannarrative

"This is my career, and I own it." Chronological story across all eleven classes. Sees gaps before anyone else does, fills evidence, holds the signed wallet. The timeline is autobiography with receipts.

Recruiterscan

"Is this person a fit, fast?" Readiness-first. Collapses to trajectory, specialty, and mobility state. Reads in 30 seconds; expands only where it matters. Optimized for triage across many candidates.

Employeraudit

"What is source-confirmed, and where are the gaps?" Tier and source on every claim, continuity checks, unexplained-gap flags. Decision posture, not decoration — the audit a credentialing committee can stand behind.

Timeline modelstructure

Axis — continuous time, anchored to each object's effective_period. Lanes — the eleven classes, collapsible into the four memory types. Marks — EvidenceObject clusters, filled by tier, hatched when stale.

As-of scrubbing — drag to any date and the timeline renders what was true then, because nothing was ever mutated. Lineage is time-travel for free.

Interactions & viewsbehavior

Filter by class, tier, or readiness context. Expand any mark to its lineage and source receipt. Switch lens — Narrative / Scan / Audit re-weights the same data without reloading it.

Views: Narrative full chronology · Scan readiness-first collapse · Audit source + gap overlay.

Architecture answer · Career ReadinessCareer Readiness is the timeline read forward. Memory looks backward at what happened; readiness asks what the accumulated evidence makes the clinician ready to do next. The same timeline that tells the story also computes the answer — which is exactly what D4 resolves.
D4

Mobility Readiness

invent portable trust

Mobility Readiness is the degree to which a clinician's Professional Memory already satisfies the evidence a target context requires — portably, without re-verification. Today, every move resets the clock: a new employer, board, or hospital re-collects and re-verifies what three others already confirmed. Readiness inverts that. Because evidence is signed, source-bound, and owned, the receiving institution accepts it rather than rebuilding it. Readiness is memory pointed at a destination.

What makes someone ready · three conditions
01 · Sufficiencycoverage

The context's gating evidence exists in memory at the required tier. A privileging committee needs primary-source license, board cert, and case logs — all present, all T3+.

02 · Currencyfreshness

Volatile evidence is inside its freshness window. A signed license observed last week is current; one 18 months stale gates readiness until refreshed.

03 · Portabilitytransfer

Evidence carries a receipt the destination can verify itself. Trust moves with the clinician instead of being rebuilt — the difference between days and minutes.

Trust portability — why this is the unlockTrust portability is the property that makes a verified fact transfer without being re-verified. It requires three things the EvidenceObject was built to carry: a signed receipt (third-party verifiable), source binding (the destination trusts the origin, not VitalCV), and ownership (the clinician has standing to present it). Where those hold, credentialing collapses from weeks of re-collection to a packet the receiver checks in minutes. What matters most, in order: primary-source license → employment continuity → board certification → malpractice history → institutional attestation.
D5

Career Evidence Graph

invent the network substrate

The Career Evidence Graph is the connective layer where every clinician, object, organization, source, and credential becomes a node — and where shared nodes turn isolated records into a network. One clinician's memory is a tree. The moment two clinicians share an employer, a board, or a specialty, those become reusable verification anchors, and trust becomes transitive. The graph is what makes VitalCV a network instead of a database.

Edge model & network effects
Relationship typesedges

holds · issued_by · employed_at · authored · verified_by · corroborates · supersedes · attests · specializes_in · recognized_by · led

Edges are themselves evidence-bearing: each carries the tier, observed_at, and receipt of the relationship it asserts. corroborates is the edge that compounds trust across independent sources.

What compoundsvalue over time

Shared anchors — one verified Source or Organization node serves every clinician attached to it. The 1,000th clinician at UCSF reuses a verification the 1st paid for.

Corroboration density — every new clinician who confirms a source strengthens it for all. Continuity — the graph remembers, so trust only ever accrues.

Architecture answerThe Career Evidence Graph is the network's memory. Nodes are entities and evidence; edges are source-bound, tiered relationships. Network effects arrive the moment a node is shared — verify the board once, trust every certification it issued; confirm the employer once, anchor every clinician who worked there. Value compounds because the graph never forgets and shared anchors are paid for once.

Network Synthesis

the buildable future state

The five deliverables are one system seen at five altitudes. Read together, they answer the wave's six questions and describe how VitalCV stops being a verification tool and becomes a Provider Career Evidence Network.

Q1What is an Evidence Object?
A single source-attributed assertion about a clinician, bound to its origin and moment of capture, content-addressed and never mutated. The atomic unit everything else is a view over.
Q2What is Professional Memory?
The durable, append-only ledger of a clinician's EvidenceObjects across eleven classes — captured once, sorted into identity, history, reputation, and trust, never re-derived.
Q3What is Career Readiness?
Professional Memory read forward: what the accumulated evidence makes a clinician ready to do next, computed from the same timeline that tells the story.
Q4What is Mobility Readiness?
Readiness made portable — the degree to which signed, source-bound, owned evidence satisfies a target context without re-verification. Trust that moves with the clinician.
Q5What is the Career Evidence Graph?
The connective substrate where clinicians, objects, organizations, and sources are nodes and tiered relationships are edges — and where shared anchors make trust transitive.
Q6How does VitalCV become a Provider Career Evidence Network?
When evidence is owned and portable, memory is durable, and the graph is shared, every new clinician and source strengthens the whole. The network is the compounding asset — and the Career Wallet is the clinician's key to it.

Build the EvidenceObject correctly — owned, source-bound, immutable, portable — and Professional Memory, the Timeline, Mobility, the Wallet, and the Network are all views and traversals over it. We did not optimize for today's credentialing workflow. We designed the object that makes every future product cheap to build and impossible to fake.
