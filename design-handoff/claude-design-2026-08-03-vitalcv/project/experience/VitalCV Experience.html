Why VitalCV exists

A clinician’s identity is rebuilt from scratch at every door they walk through.

A licensed physician changes jobs and waits, on average, fourteen weeks before billing under their own name. Not because anyone doubts them — because every employer re-collects, re-verifies, and re-doubts the same federal facts the last one already confirmed.

The credential is real. The license is real. The board certification is real. What’s broken is that none of it is portable. The proof is trapped inside whichever portal happened to gather it last.

We didn’t set out to build credentialing software. We set out to make a career provable — once, and forever.

So VitalCV asks the federal source a question, signs the answer, and hands the receipt to the clinician. Every milestone after compounds on that one verified chain — and any organization, any regulator, any year later, can replay it without us.
