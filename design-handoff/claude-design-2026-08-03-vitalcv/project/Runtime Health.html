Failure semantics v2 · runtime health · canon

Ten failure modes. Frame-distinct. Internal failures visibly separated from external.

VCV-FAIL-001 named five external degradations (A · upstream unavailable · B · anonymous restriction · C · infrastructure outage · D · no adverse findings · E · issuer unavailable). v2 adds five internal-runtime modes — F · runtime divergence, G · deployment failure, H · signer failure, J · replay failure, K · trust failure. Every mode is non-confusable by frame style alone. Subject-finding modes (A · B · D) are visibly separated from runtime-integrity modes (F · G · H · J · K). The system never forges σ to hide degradation; degraded variants are typed, owned, and named on the artifact.

02 ·

Severity ladder

4 levels · monotonic ink weight · no colour semantics
S0
SUCCESS OR ZERO-IMPACT — verified outcome rendered as a typed contract. Includes D (verified absence) and B (restricted with no leak, no σ forged). Not a failure. Frame is solid or outline; no degradation indicator; no recovery owner named because none is needed.
S1
DEGRADED · CONTINUITY HELD — σ chain continues, subject claim unaltered, freshness reduced. A (upstream unavailable) is the canonical S1. Verdict renders stale-but-signed. Recovery anchor named on the next receipt. Frame is dashed.
S2
OUTAGE · CONTAINED — plane is unavailable but the σ chain has not forked and prior receipts remain offline-verifiable. C, E, G, K live here. Frame is inverted (C · E) or double-ruled (G · K). Status URL named. Backup paths declared (E quorum, G rollback target, K cached DID mirrors).
S3
HALT · INTEGRITY THREAT — issuance is stopped to prevent forging σ. F, H, J live here. Frame is inverted with explicit halt glyph (σ ⊘ · fork · ×). On-call paged. No new σ generated under any condition until integrity is restored and audit replayed. The system would rather visibly halt than silently degrade.

07 ·

Composition rules · canon obligations

how v2 modes compose with the lineage rail · chain · receipt
Holding the chain
  • σ is never forged. Under F · H · J the system halts. No new σ is generated until the integrity contract is restored.
  • σ chain is hash-linked across deploys. G (deployment failure) does not fork the chain; rollback continues at the prior σ. New build co-signs cutover.
  • σ chain is hash-linked across rotations. Every key rotation co-signs the cutover receipt with both old and new kid for one chain step.
  • TSA anchor is independent. Mode J recovery re-derives the chain head from TSA, not from VitalCV's own logs.
Naming the degradation
  • Every degraded variant types its mode (A · B · C · D · E · F · G · H · J · K) on the artifact itself, never as a generic warning.
  • Every degraded variant names its owner. Hospital reviewers see "CMS PECOS · 47 m" or "VitalCV signer plane · backup armed" — not "issue detected."
  • Every degraded variant names its recovery. Refresh anchor, rollback target, quorum keys, forensic CLI invocation, registry liaison.
  • D is rendered as success, not as a near-miss. Verified-zero is the system's most institutionally distinctive contract.
