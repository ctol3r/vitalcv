Convergence

how the nine compose
Every trust surface composes from this vocabulary alone. Surfaces choose which primitives to render and in what order — they do not invent. 
[09 TrustStateBand]                   ← rendered when state ≠ signed
[01 TrustHeader]                      ← always, mandatory reading order

  └─ uses [03 TierBadge] · [04 OwnershipState] · [07 CheckedAtStamp]
[06 IssuerAttribution]                ← every signed surface
