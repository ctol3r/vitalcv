Technical brief · System-level delay analysis
System-level delay is dominated by upstream state-board latency and sequential manual verification cycles. These failure modes are not operator errors — they are structural properties of the existing credentialing infrastructure, documented independently by MGMA, AMA, and NCQA.

VitalCV acts as an immutable notarization buffer: federal evidence is captured once, signed at source, and reused downstream. This prevents repetitive primary re-collection — the root cause of both friction modes described above. When NPPES, OIG/LEIE, and PECOS evidence is already attested, state-board resubmissions and manual quality audits operate on a pre-verified substrate, reducing their cascade impact from full pipeline stalls to isolated, parallel events.
