Where the savings concentrate

three personas · two with disproportionate relief
Persona 01 · High relief Cost ceiling
Procedural subspecialists
Every day a cardiologist or endoscopist isn't billing carries an $8k+ revenue hit and a high probability of a locum bridge. Multi-state DEA + payer enrollment is the chokepoint — exactly the chain VitalCV runs in parallel.

Persona 02 · High relief Coverage risk
Rural & FQHC practitioners
A single delayed credentialing event closes a panel and triggers HRSA reporting risk for an FQHC. There's rarely a locum to bridge — patients reroute hours away. VitalCV's reusable evidence is the difference between an open clinic and a closed one.

General · employed
Hospitalists, internists, PAs on hospital salary. Recovery is real but bounded — payroll continues regardless, and locum is often optional. Still: a hospitalist credentialed two months earlier is a hospitalist accreting ~$150k in lost-revenue avoidance.

What remains manual

explicit limits · institutional maturity
VitalCV does not automate these (yet) · they are humans on the other end v3.2 · reviewed 2026-04-30
01
State Board outreach
PSV requests to non-API state boards — still email/phone
02
Malpractice carrier confirmations
No standard API — carrier-by-carrier human follow-up
03
Hospital committee scheduling
CVO writes the dossier; cadence is owned by the hospital
04
Reference letter back-and-forth
VitalCV stores returned letters; the chase is still email-on-email
05
DEA modifications
Federal form submission — we track status, we don't file for you
06
Privileging committee minutes / appeals
Governance artifact — outside the credentialing pipeline by design
07
Out-of-network payer enrollments
In-network covered via PECOS reuse — OON is a manual lane
08
CME audit reconciliation
We surface gaps; we do not generate certificates retroactively
Implementation note. The boundary above is intentional. VitalCV replaces the busywork around credentialing — not the human function itself. Anything that needs judgment, scheduling, or non-API negotiation stays where the relationships already live: with your CVO, your committee chairs, and your payer reps. We surface state and write the audit row when the human work completes.
