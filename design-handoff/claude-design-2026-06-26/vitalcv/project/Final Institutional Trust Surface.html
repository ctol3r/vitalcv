One reading model. Four surfaces. 30 seconds to trust.

A hospital verifier loading any of /verify, /trust, /receipt, or /replay arrives at a conclusion within 30 seconds. Every trust-bearing fact is visibly attributable to its source. Every replay chain is visibly inspectable. Every issuer is visibly identifiable. Every signed state is externally verifiable via offline CLI against an independent TSA anchor.
