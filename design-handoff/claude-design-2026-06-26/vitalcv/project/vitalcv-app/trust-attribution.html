NPDB · National Practitioner Data Bank

npdb.hrsa.gov · institution-gated · we do not read this on a clinician's behalf
Institution-onlyout of scope

VitalCV does not read NPDB. The Healthcare Quality Improvement Act restricts who may query the data bank. The institution itself must perform this read directly. VitalCV surfaces "Institution must read" on every passport — never an empty checkmark.
