Remaining SaaS-like elements
  • Continuity strip metric sizing (18 px monospace for "14 records," "1 gap," "6.4 d avg") — borderline dashboard register. FAA logbooks render these as inline counts, not summary tiles. Consider compressing to a single sentence header above the table.
  • "σ-rate · 8 / 8" right-column on lane chart — reads as analytics. Maintenance logs name *exceptions*, not rates. Consider showing only deviations (e.g. "1 stale at W-1") and dropping the perfect rows.
  • "head week shaded" annotation in lane cap — pedagogy chrome. Remove; the head cell renders distinctly enough.
  • Three different gap renderings across the file: dashed border (ledger table), dashed-dot custody node, em-dash cell (lane). Unify to one. Pick the dashed-dot custody node — it is the most evidentiary.
  • "σ-rate" column header itself — the term "rate" is analytics vocabulary. Rename to "σ verdict / 8" or "exceptions."
