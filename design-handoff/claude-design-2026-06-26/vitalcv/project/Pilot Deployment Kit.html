01

Executive summary — in three paragraphs.

D43 · S01

VitalCV is a read-only verification node. It does not replace a credentialing verification organization. It resolves the eight federal primary sources Cedar's CVO already resolves — only faster, with a signed, replayable receipt for each clinician — and surfaces the boundary where state-level and clinical-privileging work remains a manual administrative step.

For this pilot, we are proposing a fixed cohort of ten provisional NPIs, intake'd through Cedar's existing Okta tenant, processed through VitalCV's federal resolver, and returned to Cedar as a side-channel artifact for comparison against your CVO's output. The pilot generates one signed receipt per clinician, hosted at a single revocable URL.

We are not asking Cedar to change a single line of its credentialing workflow during the pilot window. We are asking for thirty days to demonstrate that federal-source resolution can be accelerated by an order of magnitude without sacrificing audit fidelity — and to define, jointly, what a production engagement would look like if the receipts hold up.

02

Pilot scope — what is in, what is explicitly out.

D43 · S02

The pilot is bounded on four axes: cohort, surface, data, time. Each axis is enumerated below. Items in the right column are not deferred — they are categorically outside the pilot's responsibility.

In scope10 NPIs / 30 days
  • +Identity resolution against NPPES and PECOS, returning normalized name, taxonomy, enrollment status.
  • +Sanction screening against OIG/LEIE and SAM.gov, returning a binary clear/excluded on intake date.
  • +DEA registration resolution with expiration and schedule grants.
  • +NPDB self-query token minting, returned to the clinician for verifier proxy.
  • +One signed receipt per clinician, resolvable from a Cedar-revocable URL.
  • +Read-only replay surface for Cedar's audit team, scoped to the pilot cohort.
Out of scopeverifier responsibility
  • −State medical board PSV. Resolved manually by Cedar's CVO or a partner CVO operating under their own credential.
  • −Privileging decisions. No assertion is made about scope of practice or clinical privileges at any Cedar facility.
  • −Employment eligibility. I-9, work authorization, OFAC screening remain in Cedar's HRIS workflow.
  • −Malpractice adjudication. NPDB query results are the verifier's to read; VitalCV mints the token, never the determination.
  • −Re-credentialing cycles. The pilot covers initial verification only; re-credentialing remains in Cedar's CVO cadence.
  • −Integration with Cedar's EMR, HRIS, or scheduling systems. No upstream writes; pilot artifacts are side-channel only.

The pilot ends on day 30. On termination, all subject records are cryptographically deleted from VitalCV's pilot cluster within 72 hours, with a signed deletion receipt issued to Cedar. Pilot URLs revoke immediately on Cedar's instruction at any point in the window.

03

Security posture — what data is touched, what isn't.

D43 · S03

VitalCV's pilot architecture is structurally incapable of touching Cedar PHI or Cedar HRIS records. The table below enumerates every data class the pilot can encounter and the posture under which it is handled.

Data classTouched?Handling posture
NPI numberYesPublic federal identifier. Stored as the run's subject id. Discarded with cohort at day-30 deletion.
Clinician legal nameYesRead from NPPES on resolution; never accepted from Cedar's HRIS. Retained only in the signed receipt during pilot window.
Date of birthNoNot requested at intake. Federal sources used in the pilot do not require DOB for resolution.
Social Security NumberNoCategorically out of scope. VitalCV will refuse SSN if Cedar's HRIS adapter attempts to send it.
Patient PHINoVitalCV does not connect to any Cedar clinical system. PHI cannot reach the pilot cluster by design — no integration surface exists.
Cedar HRIS recordsNoPilot accepts only the NPI list from Cedar's credentialing team. HRIS connectors are explicitly disabled in the pilot tenant.
DEA registrationYesResolved against the public DEA registration registry; only registration class, status, and expiration retained.
NPDB query resultsNoVitalCV mints the subject's NPDB self-query token. The query result is returned directly to the verifier, never persisted by VitalCV.
  1. Issuer keyAll pilot receipts are signed under did:web:vitalcv.health, ed25519 key fingerprint 4f2a, rotated quarterly. Public key resolvable from the issuer's well-known endpoint at any time.
  2. Network posturePilot cluster operates on a single-tenant container, isolated from any production workload, behind a Cloudflare tunnel revocable by Cedar with a 5-minute SLA.
  3. EncryptionAll subject records encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM); transport TLS 1.3 only. Per-record keys derived from a pilot-cohort master key, destroyed on day-30 termination.
  4. AuditEvery read, write, and signing operation is logged to an append-only audit ledger, exportable to Cedar's audit team in CSV or JSON-LD on request.
  5. TerminationCedar may terminate the pilot at any point. Termination triggers immediate revocation of all pilot URLs, followed by cryptographic deletion within 72 hours and a signed deletion receipt.

  1. No HRIS write-backThe pilot is read-only with respect to Cedar systems. VitalCV emits artifacts; Cedar reads them. There is no upstream integration to be hardened, audited, or rolled back.
  2. Subject-controlled shareThe clinician — not VitalCV, not Cedar — holds the vault key. Cedar's access is mediated by the subject's share grant and is revocable by either party.
  3. Receipts, not databasesThe unit of trust is the signed receipt, not a row in our database. Receipts are portable, replayable, and survive VitalCV's eventual sunset.
05

30-day success criteria — what we measure, jointly.

D43 · S05

Four criteria. Each has an operational target measured against Cedar's current CVO baseline. We pass the pilot if we hit three of four; we discuss continuation if we hit fewer.

Criterion 01Time-to-receipt
Median time from intake to signed receipt across the cohort.
Cedar CVO baseline for federal-source resolution: 4–6 business days. VitalCV pilot target: under one hour.
Target≤ 1 h
Criterion 02Source fidelity
Receipt fields must reconcile to Cedar CVO output on independent review.
Cedar's audit team blind-compares VitalCV receipts to CVO records. Zero substantive discrepancies across the cohort.
Target0 / 10
Criterion 03Operator load
Minutes of Cedar-team time required to operate the pilot.
Total hands-on minutes from Cedar credentialing across the 30 days, excluding the audit-comparison work in criterion 02.
Target≤ 90 min
Criterion 04Audit defensibility
Cedar audit lead can defend the receipt to a regulator on a single read.
Tested on day 28 with a structured walk-through of a randomly chosen cohort receipt. Pass criterion: no follow-up artifacts required.
Target1-shot
  1. What we will not measureNet Promoter Score. Engagement minutes. "Time saved" estimates without a baseline. We do not run feel-good metrics in a credentialing pilot.
  2. What constitutes a "no"Cedar passes on continuation if any single criterion drops below half its target. We will not negotiate the threshold mid-pilot.
  3. What constitutes a "yes"Cedar and VitalCV scope a 90-day production engagement covering 50–100 NPIs and a joint definition of when state-board PSV moves in-platform under a partner CVO umbrella.
