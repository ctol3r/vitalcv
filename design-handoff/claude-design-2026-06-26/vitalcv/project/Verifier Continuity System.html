Canonical six-primitive system · verifier continuity

Six primitives. One reading order. Three trust states.

The verifier-side reading system. Six primitives (canonical header, replay timeline, issuer continuity, provenance strip, continuity indicators, degraded taxonomy) compose every verifier-class surface in VitalCV. One reading order (OBJECT → OWNERSHIP → CHECKED_AT → CHANNEL → REPLAY → RUN_ID) is taught by the tick rail and held across all surfaces. Three trust states (signed · snapshot · preview) carry the artifact register. No new vocabulary may be introduced. The system is closed.

Reading contract

The issuer continuity panel answers "is the signer of this attestation operationally continuous over a meaningful window?". Five mandatory rows: primary kid (with in-service bar + rotation horizon), DID document state, status registry state, backup quorum state, conformance regime.

Rotation bar uses the system's only progress indicator — fill = days in service against rotation horizon. now and rotate · Nd labels render endpoint annotation on first encounter. The bar may be omitted on dense composed surfaces; the kid age value must remain.

This is the primitive that distinguishes VitalCV from comparator credentialing tools: most surfaces render the current signature; this surface renders the operational discipline behind it.

Contract
Provenance strip renders four roles, not four sources. Roles are: Signer (issuer of attestation), Authority (primary-source domain for each named claim), Anchor (independent trust anchor for replay). Sources may number 2–6; roles always number exactly 4. Eyebrow numbering (01–04) is mandatory — names the role enumeration. Role pill at bottom of each cell is mandatory — semantically separates signer from authority on the page.

Disambiguation contract

Five modes are visually non-confusable by frame alone. A reviewer never needs to read prose to classify the mode. Three confusion pairs are protected by the system:

D ≠ A — D has 2 px solid frame + filled check + count pill. A has dashed paper + chevron. Frame weight is categorical. C ≠ E — C is ink-900 with square glyph + names VitalCV. E is ink-950 with diamond glyph + names the issuer cryptographic plane. Glyph delta is categorical. A ≠ B — A is dashed (upstream); B is solid (policy gate). Frame solidity is categorical.

D is the most important contract: verified absence ≠ failure. The 2 px frame, the filled check on ink-950, the affirmative-negative token, and the inline count pill all encode success. No comparator class renders verified absence as a first-class outcome; VitalCV does.

Six primitives compose every verifier-class surface. Three trust states (signed · snapshot · preview) carry the artifact register. Five degraded modes are non-confusable by frame alone. The vocabulary is closed; the next pass is application.
What this system delivers
  • Canonical header — 6 slots, tick rail, monotonic order, three trust-state renderings.
  • Replay timeline — inverted plane, σ-pill per node, gap typed, TSA-anchored, three render densities.
  • Issuer continuity panel — 5-row signer-continuity card with key-rotation horizon.
  • Provenance strip — 4-cell role enumeration: Signer · Authority · Authority · Anchor.
  • Continuity indicators — three state stamps + four replay sub-chips.
  • Degraded taxonomy — five non-confusable modes, D rendered as success.
What no surface may do
  • Introduce a 7th type size, a 7th lineage slot, a 6th degraded mode, a 4th trust state, or a 4th ownership state.
  • Use colour as a semantic channel (single ink axis only).
  • Render verified absence as a failure mode (D ≠ A).
  • Collapse σ-defer into σ-ok or σ-fail (gap is its own class).
  • Render the issuer DID without separating it from the authority domain (issuer ≠ authority).
  • Omit the TSA anchor or offline CLI from a replay timeline footer.
