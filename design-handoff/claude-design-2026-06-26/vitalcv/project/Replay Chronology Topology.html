Chronology topology · canon

Every receipt is a node. Every chain reconciles to one TSA anchor.

A chronology is not a list — it is a topology. Per-subject σ-chains are visibly distinct and never cross, every receipt is positioned by checked_at, every σ defer (mode A) is visibly notched into the line, every recovery anchor is named, every issuer rotation co-signs across both subject chains it crosses, and every chain head reconciles to the same independent RFC 3161 TSA anchor. A verifier sees the entire 90-day history without ambiguity about who signed what and when.

Topology obligations · canon

what the chain must be · what it must not be
Must holdpositive obligations
  • Linear per subject. Each subject's chain is a strict sequence. No branching, no merges, no out-of-order writes.
  • Hash-linked. Every receipt names its predecessor's hash. The link is verifiable without the issuer present.
  • σ-typed at every node. Every receipt declares σ ok, σ defer, or σ withheld. There is no fourth state.
  • Recovery anchor named. Every σ defer is followed by an explicit recovery receipt whose recovers field names the deferred receipt.
  • Rotation co-signed. Every key rotation produces one receipt signed by both old kid and new kid. This is the cross-key continuity proof.
  • TSA on every node. RFC 3161 counter-signature on every receipt. Independent key custody from issuer.
Must not occurprohibited states
  • No forks. Two receipts naming the same prev-hash is a mode J halt. Issuance stops until forensic re-derivation completes.
  • No retro-edits. A receipt's bytes are immutable from σ-time forward. Corrections are issued as new receipts that name the receipt they supersede.
  • No silent gaps. A missed cadence tick is itself a receipt (σ defer · mode A or E) — not the absence of a receipt.
  • No cross-subject leakage. A receipt under subject A never references claims of subject B. Lanes never cross.
  • No unsigned origin. The first receipt in every subject chain is admit-signed. There is no implicit subject.
  • No forged σ to hide degradation. Under any failure mode the system renders the typed mode, never a synthetic "fresh" verdict.
