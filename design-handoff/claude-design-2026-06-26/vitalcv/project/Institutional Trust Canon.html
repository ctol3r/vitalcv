08 ·

Typography ramp + spacing hierarchy

four sizes · four spacings · monospace identifiers
Type ramp
  • --t-eyebrow · 9.5 px — keys, monospace labels, eyebrow text. Letter-spacing 0.18em, uppercase.
  • --t-meta · 10.5 px — operational metadata (sub-labels, run-time tokens). Sans, no-caps.
  • --t-body · 11.75 px — identifiers and values. Always monospace, tabular-nums.
  • --t-rule · 13 px — row text, banner titles, verdict. Sans for prose, monospace for IDs.
  • --t-title · 15 px — section heads. Sans, weight 600.
  • --t-h1 · 22 px — one h1 per document. Sans, weight 600, tracking -0.02em.

Discipline: never introduce a fifth identifier size. Never set an identifier in sans. Never use color to indicate emphasis — use weight (500 → 600) or background (paper → ink-900) instead.

Spacing
  • --sp-1 · 4 px — within-cell vertical gap (key → value).
  • --sp-2 · 8 px — chip gaps, between paired tokens.
  • --sp-3 · 12 px — cell padding (row to row).
  • --sp-4 · 16 px — section gutter, primitive padding.
  • --sp-5 · 20 px — between adjacent primitives in a frame.
  • --sp-6 · 24 px — masthead padding.
  • --sp-8 · 32 px — between major sections.

Baseline: 4 px grid. Every border lands on a 0.5 px subpixel — hairlines remain crisp at 1× and sharper at 2×. No shadows. No glass. No gradients.
