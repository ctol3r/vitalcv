90-Second Reviewer Script
Narrative — What the reviewer should walk away knowing
1
What VitalCV is
A trust infrastructure platform that aggregates source evidence for healthcare provider credentials and packages it into audit-ready artifacts — ready for institution review without requiring each institution to start from scratch.
2
Why now
Healthcare credentialing is a paper-intensive, institution-siloed, manually repeated process. Every hospital re-verifies the same provider from scratch. The evidence exists — it just isn't packaged for reuse. VitalCV solves the packaging problem.
3
The wedge
Start with credential evidence aggregation for individual clinicians. Clinician uploads once; institution reviewers receive a pre-assembled, source-confirmed evidence packet. Reduce review time. Build trust over time. Expand to reusable cross-institution credential portability.
4
Proof of product
Working attribution system. Evidence provenance modeled at tier depth. Receipt + dossier artifacts generatable. Clerk auth live. The infrastructure aesthetic is deliberate — this is designed for institutional trust, not consumer SaaS.
5
Founder insight
The attribution boundary model and bounded audit semantics show deep familiarity with how credentialing institutions think about evidence provenance. This isn't a feature — it's a philosophy baked into the product architecture.
6
Pilot path
The company is in pilot phase. The right next step is a conversation about a specific institution or employer running a pilot cohort. VitalCV is designed to be reviewable and auditable from day one.
