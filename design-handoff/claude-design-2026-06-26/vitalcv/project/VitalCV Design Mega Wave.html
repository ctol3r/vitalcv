01

Design Doctrine

binding on all surfaces

Core thesis: VitalCV makes credential evidence legible — not verified. The interface must never imply more certainty than the underlying source data provides. Every design decision is an epistemic act.

PrincipleRuleForbidden opposite
Evidence-firstShow what is source-confirmed, what is missing, what requires access. Always.Vague "verified" badges with no source attribution.
Institutional honestyEvery surface names its epistemic limits. VitalCV does not replace human credentialing review.Copy implying automatic trust transfer or institutional acceptance.
Calm typographyGeist (narrative) + Geist Mono (data/identity). No other faces. No decorative type.Mixed fonts, italics for emphasis, all-caps in body copy.
Border semanticsStatus is encoded in border style: solid=confirmed, dotted=pending, dashed=stale/gap, none=unknown.Color fills as the primary status signal. Opacity for degraded data.
Inverted registerDark/inverted surfaces reserved for cryptographic plane: receipts, signatures, lineage strips.Inverted surfaces for hero panels, marketing blocks, or KPI cards.
Reading orderObject → Ownership → checked_at → Channel → Replay → run_id. Never reordered.generated_by before checked_at. Source after timestamp.
Warm minimalismPaper background (#f4f2ec), ink scale, hairline rules. Space is a design element.Gradient backgrounds. Shadow-heavy cards. Glossy UI treatments.
Agentic UXAny automated action shows: what will happen, why, confidence level, interrupt option, and audit trail.Silent automation. "AI is handling this." Invisible agent actions.
Skeleton screensLoading = skeleton placeholders matching final layout. No spinners.Spinning loaders. Progress bars without estimated time. "Loading..." text.
Status tablesOperational facts in tables with source, timestamp, freshness. Not marketing adjectives.Animated counters. Big-number KPI cards. Dashboard tropes.

Approved visual vocabulary

Typography
Geist 300–700 for narrative. Geist Mono 400–600 for all data, IDs, labels, timestamps. Zero other faces.
Color model
Single ink axis. OKLCH status tints only for state (not decoration). Paper + white surfaces. Warm neutral background.
Motion
150ms status rows. 200ms page fade. 300ms hero. No looping animation. No particles. prefers-reduced-motion: none at all.
Spacing
4px base unit. Generous vertical rhythm. Max 72ch prose width. Dense tables acceptable for operational surfaces.

Permanently prohibited

PatternWhy prohibited
Gradient backgroundsReads as generic SaaS. Conflicts with evidence-first posture.
Emoji in product UIHealthcare trust infrastructure. None. The emoji bar is zero.
Left-border accent containersOverused trope. Status is in border style, not in left-side color strips.
Progress rings / donut chartsImplies a percentage model. No canonical derivation exists for credential completeness %.
SVG-drawn imageryUse placeholders. Bad SVG illustrations degrade trust signal.
Particle / graph animationsDecorative theater. The graph explains provenance, it does not entertain.
"AI-powered" / "intelligent"Describes tech, not outcome. Say what the system does, not how.
Standards as marketing copy"VC 2.0 compatible" in a trust strip is the single line most likely to discount the whole surface to an institutional reader. Link to conformance assertions instead.

Trust Graph Visual System

provenance · not decoration

The Trust Graph explains provenance. It is not a visualization of system health or AI capability. A reviewer should understand it in 10 seconds: clinician at center, sources as satellites, evidence as smaller nodes, gaps as dashed nodes. Dense enough to show the network. Sparse enough to read it.

Node taxonomy · 10 types

01 · Identity
Clinician
Center node. Largest. Dark fill + inner ring. NPI label below.
02 · Source Authority
NPPES, DEA, OIG
Medium circles. Border color = status. No fill or status-tinted fill.
03 · Credential
License, DEA cert
Smaller circle. Confirmed = solid border + sage tint.
04 · Artifact
PDF, scan
Rounded rectangle. Document metaphor. Smaller than credential.
05 · Receipt
Anchored receipt
Square. σ glyph inside. Inverted if signed plane.
06 · Institution
Cedar Health
Double-ring, outer dashed = review in progress. Larger than source.
07 · Review Action
Reviewed · operator
Diamond. Confirmed = sage border. Pending = amber dotted border.
08 · Gap / Blocker
Blocker
Large dashed circle. Terracotta stroke. No fill or faint tint only.
09 · Stale / Expired
Stale source
Dotted-dashed circle. Muted amber/terracotta. Never opacity-dimmed.
10 · Access-Required
Auth needed
Dotted circle. Indigo stroke. Lock icon overlay optional.

Passport · /passport

clinician-facing credential wallet

The passport is a portable, source-backed packet. It must feel less like a resume and more like a credential wallet with visible provenance. Critical: Every claim row must show ownership badge (subject/delegated/unbound) and the signer must appear above the fold. A passport without a visible signer is a screenshot, not a credential.

SectionComponentsRequired fieldsDefects to avoid
Identity headerMasthead: name, NPI (mono), taxonomy, location, readiness posture chipNPI in Geist Mono + tabular-nums. Posture chip visible above fold.NPI in sans-serif. Posture below fold. Signer absent.
Credential cardsLicense, NPI, enrollment, certs, safety checks, uploaded artifactsEach card: claim, ownership badge, source, checked_at stack, artifact link.Missing ownership badge. Opacity-dimmed stale cards. No action button on gaps.
Evidence timelineVertical chain: source queried → result received → artifact generated → receipt anchored → shared → review requestedISO timestamp per event. Custodian label per event. Chain head visible.Analytics-style timeline. No timestamps. Chain rendered as progress bar.
Share panelShare packet, copy secure link, export audit bundle, request refreshSelective disclosure: show what will be shared, what will not, recipient, expiration.No disclosure panel. "Share all" with no granularity. SSI jargon.
Trust Graph viewToggle: list view ↔ graph viewIdentity at center, credentials linked, source authorities as outer nodes.Graph as default for non-technical users. No list fallback.

decision panel left · evidence right

A reviewer lands here and must know within 30 seconds: what is source-confirmed, what requires review, and what is safe to do next. Two-column desktop layout. Decision panel on left (sticky). Evidence packet on right (scrollable).

Decision actionCanonical copyWhat it impliesWhat it must NOT imply
PrimaryProceed as head startUsing this as starting point for your institution's own review.VitalCV has verified or approved this clinician.
SecondaryRequest refreshTrigger a new source query on stale or pending items.Current data is wrong or incomplete.
TertiaryRoute to human reviewFlagging for a reviewer in the institution's workflow.VitalCV is routing to its own review process.
