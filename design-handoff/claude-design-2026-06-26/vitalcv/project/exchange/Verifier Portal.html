02 How acceptance works

Four moves replace a six-week verification cycle.

The whole credentialing back-and-forth collapses into a loop you control end to end — and every step is written to your own audit ledger.

STEP 01
Request
ask the network
Request exactly the evidence you need against a professional's identity. The exchange routes it to the receipts that already exist.
STEP 02
Review
replay the proof
Replay the receipt against the original source in real time. You see the source, the signature, and a clean re-verification — not a faxed letter.
STEP 03
Accept
one click, locked
Accept the evidence. Your decision is written to your organization's audit ledger — a permanent, tamper-evident record of what you trusted and when.
STEP 04
Track freshness
never goes stale
Every receipt you hold is tracked against its source's cadence. When something ages out, you know before it costs you — and re-request in a click.
