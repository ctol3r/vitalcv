03 How trust flows

Five roles. One shared record between them.

The exchange has no center to capture. Evidence moves from the role that can prove it to the role that needs it — and the professional in the middle owns the whole chain.

STEP 01
Issuer
signs at the source
Schools, boards, hospitals, and federal registries answer one authoritative question and sign the answer. A receipt — never a screenshot.
STEP 02
Evidence
flows, replayable
The signed receipt carries the source, the question, and the timestamp — verifiable for 7 years on any machine, with no VitalCV in the loop.
STEP 03
Professional
owns the chain
Every receipt flows into one portable identity the clinician owns — not the employer, not the board, not us. They carry it everywhere, re-collecting nothing.
STEP 04
Organization
verifies by replay
Employers and payers accept evidence by replaying it against the original source — credentialing-by-proxy. A quarter of onboarding becomes days.
STEP 05
Career
compounds
Roles, panels, research, and society standing attach to the same chain. Trust stops being a gate and becomes portable, differentiated signal.
