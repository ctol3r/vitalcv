01 The ecosystem

Nine components. One trust cloud.

Each component is a layer of the same platform — and each one compounds on the one before it. Together they are the substrate every healthcare workforce application is built on.

01

Identity

the anchor

A clinician’s identity is established at the source and bound to their NPI — the single anchor every other layer attaches to, owned by the professional, never an employer.

NPI-bound · portableOpen
02

Evidence

signed at source

Every fact is a signed receipt carrying the source, the question, and the timestamp — replayable for 7 years by anyone, with no VitalCV in the loop. A receipt, never a screenshot.

Cryptographic · 7-yr replayOpen
03

Recognition

differentiated signal

Leadership, outcomes, references, research, and society standing attach to the chain — turning “verified” into the signal that distinguishes one clinician from the next.

Portable reputationOpen
04

Trust

verifiable outside the issuer

Each receipt is replayable against the original evidence on any machine — verifiability without us in the loop. Trust accumulates into evidence-weighted tiers, T1 → T4.

Replays standaloneOpen
05

Organizations

build on the record

Health systems, payers, and staffing firms read, attest to, and extend the chain — never modifying the core. They verify by replay and turn a quarter of onboarding into days.

Extends · audit-lockedOpen
06

Developers

the open surface

APIs, SDKs, and keys expose identity, evidence, and trust to any builder. Applications don’t each rebuild verification — they inherit it from the platform.

APIs · SDKs · keysOpen
07

Integrations

events & embeds

Webhooks, embeds, and connectors push trust events the moment they happen — evidence signed, trust accepted, recognition added — into every connected system in real time.

Webhooks · embedsOpen
08

Exchange

the shared network

One network with no center to capture, where issuers sign, professionals own, and verifiers accept by replay. Trust, once issued, flows downstream untouched — re-collected by no one.

09

Career

compounds over decades

The outcome of every layer: a career that compounds instead of repeating. Across every transition — student to executive — the same chain carries forward, re-verified at no move.
