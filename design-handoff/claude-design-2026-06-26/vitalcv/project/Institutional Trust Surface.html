What you can verify without us
Resolve did:web:vitalcv.health, fetch the jwks, validate the signature on a presented credential, and check the status bit. Our server is not in the verification path. If we are offline, your verification still completes; if we are compromised, our revocation procedure flips every affected credential's status bit within 15 minutes.
What requires our cooperation
Receipt-chain inspection past the badge itself (audit replay), source-registry re-resolution at the moment of issuance, and degraded-state history on a specific receipt. None of these are required to trust a current credential — they are forensic and historical only.
