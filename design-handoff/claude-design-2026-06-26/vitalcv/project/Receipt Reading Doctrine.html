Receipt reading doctrine · audit-grade artifact contract

A VitalCV receipt is a signed operational manifest — not a confirmation page.

This document codifies how a VitalCV receipt must read to a credentialing director, an NCQA file reviewer, or an auditor in cryptographic verification. Twelve rules. One scan-order trace. Five degraded receipt variants. The rules govern application; the canon (Trust Primitives) governs vocabulary.
01 ·

Twelve rules of receipt reading

doctrine · binding on every receipt surface
R-01 · Verdict before content

Trust must establish before claim prose renders.

The verdict pill, receipt-ID, and state stamp occupy the head row. No subject content reads above them. A reviewer who stops reading at the head row must still leave with a defensible verdict.

outcome · 0.8 s to verdict
R-02 · Identity is triplicated

Receipt-ID renders three times — head, lineage, chain.

Receipt-ID renders at the head row, again on the lineage rail as run_id, and again on the chain-of-custody as the head node. Three independent confirmations of the same evidentiary anchor.

outcome · re-derivation possible from any one
R-03 · Issuer ≠ authority

VitalCV signs the attestation. It does not own the underlying facts.

The sealed-attestation paragraph names the issuer in monospace and the sources in separated KV cells. The distinction is stated in plain English on the artifact, not in a footnote.

outcome · provenance unambiguous
R-04 · Cryptographic plane is inverted

Signed-tone surfaces render ink-950 with white identifiers.

Chain-of-custody, state stamp, verdict pill on a signed receipt — all render inverted. Holder-side and source-side surfaces render on paper. Tone signals trust plane, not aesthetic.

outcome · plane legible at 1 m
R-05 · Six slots, fixed order

OBJECT → OWNERSHIP → CHECKED_AT → CHANNEL → REPLAY → RUN_ID.

Every lineage rail renders these six in this order. A reviewer learns the order once, applies it everywhere. The tick rail beneath the cells (01–06) teaches the order without explanation.

outcome · taught surface · learned once
R-06 · Timestamps are ISO 8601 UTC

Z-suffix on the artifact. Sans "X ago" beneath, for humans.

Monospace ISO 8601 with Z is the auditable form. Sans relative time is the reviewer's aid. Both render. Neither replaces the other.

outcome · forensic + human in one cell
R-07 · Gaps are named, never elided

A missing record is a dashed empty record with named cause and owner.

Continuity is asserted by rendering what is absent, not by skipping over it. Every gap carries an upstream owner (CMS, OIG, board) and a recovery anchor (next σ-ok node).

outcome · degradation auditable
R-08 · σ is per-node, not per-receipt

Every evidentiary record carries its own σ verdict.

A receipt with one σ-defer node and thirteen σ-ok nodes is not "signed." It is "13 of 14 signed · 1 deferred." σ is per-node so degradation cannot hide inside a summary.

outcome · σ-defer cannot collapse into success
R-09 · Re-verification on the artifact

Every receipt names its offline re-derivation path.

The footer carries the verify URL and the CLI invocation. vitalcv-verify --offline. The artifact survives skepticism without VitalCV in the room.

outcome · re-derivable offline
R-10 · No celebratory affordances

"Verified" is a fact, not a milestone.

No check-mark icons in paper register. No green. No animation. No "Success!" toast. The state stamp's typographic weight does the work. Celebration breaks the audit register.

outcome · no SaaS confirmation chrome
R-11 · Mode D is success, not absence

"0 records · no adverse findings" reads as affirmative negative.

Heavy 2 px border. Filled check glyph. Inline count pill (0 RECORDS). Success token in the eyebrow. A reviewer cannot mistake Mode D for Mode A.

outcome · D ≠ A under speed
R-12 · Document control before content

Form number, revision, effective date, controller, classification render first.

The artifact is under document control before it is in use. NCQA · Federal Register · FAA logbook precedent. Reviewer establishes regulated-artifact register in the first 2 seconds.

outcome · institutional register · 2 s

Receipt visual maturity verdict

comparator alignment · audit-grade artifact register
Comparator alignment · where this register sits
  • Regulated audit artifacts (NCQA HC-1, Joint Commission file review) — form-numbered, revision-stamped, controller named, attestation paragraph, sources separately attributed. ✓ matched: document-control band + sealed-attestation prose + source KV cells.
  • Chain-of-custody records (DEA Form 41) — every transfer signed, no record elision, named recipient. ✓ matched: chain-of-custody render at every receipt, σ pill at every node, gap-named with named owner.
  • Signed operational manifests (Sigstore Cosign, SLSA provenance docs) — issuer DID, signing key fingerprint, content hash, status, re-derivation invocation. ✓ matched: cryptographic 4-column block + verify CLI on footer.
  • Cryptographic trust documents (RFC 3161 tokens, transparency-log inclusion proofs) — TSA anchor, inclusion path, head hash, offline re-verification. ✓ matched: TSA anchor on footer, chain-of-custody as inclusion proof, --offline flag exposed.

Verdict: a VitalCV receipt now reads as the intersection of these four artifact classes — not as any one of them, but as the class that subsumes their evidentiary contracts.

Remaining SaaS-like receipt patterns
  • Three buttons on the live verify-foot (Verify now · Download VC · Print) — confirmation-page pattern. Reduce to Verify now + Copy CLI. The CLI is the actual audit affordance.
  • "≈ 30 s read time" annotation on the verdict bar — UX metric in evidentiary chrome. Pedagogy belongs on doctrine; not on the artifact itself. Remove from production receipt.
  • State-stamp duplication — currently renders in document-control band and in head row. Head-row is canonical (pairs with verdict). Remove from document-control band.
  • "V" glyph on document-control band — the only piece of decorative branding on otherwise regulated chrome. Replace with form number rendered as 24×24 monospace cell.
  • Inset double-border + perforation strip on the live receipt frame — printed-certificate ornament. Remove both. Single 1.5 px outer perimeter; single hairline section breaks.
  • "Verifiable" rendered at 18 px monospace bold — celebratory size jump. Drop to 15 px matching state-stamp weight. Federal Register equivalents render verdict and state stamp at the same scale.

Audit-grade
A VitalCV receipt is a signed operational manifest. Strongest surface: Mode D — the only register in this comparator set that renders verified absence of adverse findings as a first-class success, not as null state.
Strongest audit-grade surface

§03 · Mode D · no adverse findings. The doctrine's hardest test is whether 0 records reads as a verified outcome rather than as absence of data. NCQA reviewers, OIG auditors, and credentialing directors all know the difference; most SaaS dashboards do not. Mode D renders the affirmative negative with: heavy 2 px artifact border · "Verifiable · 0 records" verdict pill · ink-900 σ-ok pills against the OIG query line · sealed-attestation prose that states "this is not absence of data. this is verified absence of adverse findings" in plain English on the artifact · and a footer that names the expected re-verification outcome (--offline · expect σ ok · expect count 0). A reviewer cannot mistake Mode D for Mode A. The contract is held at the pixel level. This is the receipt class that proves VitalCV reads as audit infrastructure, not as a verification SaaS.
