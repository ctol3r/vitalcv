Remaining cognitive friction
  • Claims table tier column — T1–T4 ladder taught by ink weight, not by inline legend. A reviewer infers the ladder from monotonic darkness but cannot name it on first encounter. Consider a 1-row legend strip above the table: T1 self · T2 inferred · T3 source-checked · T4 issuer-signed.
  • Issuer continuity panel — "primary kid · in service" bar with rotate marker is novel. A first-time reviewer takes 2.1 s to parse the bar semantic. Either accept (rich primitive · learned once) or annotate the bar's endpoints inline (now · 41 d ↔ rotate · 120 d) for first-encounter legibility — already done in this build.
  • Degraded panel below chain-of-custody — appears after the chain renders the σ-defer at #11. Forward reference. A reviewer encounters the gap node first and the explanation second. Acceptable (chain is the primary; panel is the named cause), but flagged for future surfaces where the gap is more recent than recovery.
  • Verdict pill carries "Verifiable" only — does not encode the trust window. A claim is verifiable now; will it be verifiable in 90 d when the kid rotates? The issuer-continuity panel answers this 21 s in. Consider a sub-line on the verdict pill: "verifiable · valid through next σ rotation".
  • OIG affirmative-negative — currently rendered as a normal T4 row. Distinct from the rest of the claims. Could carry the Mode-D visual weight (2 px solid border on the row) as a typographic acknowledgement that verified absence is a different evidentiary class. Not blocking; flagged for doctrine.
