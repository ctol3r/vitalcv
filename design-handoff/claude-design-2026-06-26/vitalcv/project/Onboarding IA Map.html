Onboarding IA · four zones · explicit boundaries

What is public. What is authenticated. What requires identity proof.

The system is four zones, not one app. Each zone has its own URL, its own auth requirement, and its own trust boundary. A clinician should never be in doubt about which zone they are in — the chrome, the boundary chip, and the URL all agree.

Public ≠ identified
Zone A is intentionally anonymous. Marketing copy, sample artifacts, source registry list. If a clinician sees their own data, they are not in Zone A.
Authenticated ≠ identity-proven
Zone B says: this person controls an inbox at your IdP. It does not say this person is the lawful holder of any NPI. Zone C does that binding.
Onboarded ≠ credentialed
Zone C produces a Trust Badge — a snapshot of source-backed lanes at issuance. It is an input to your credentialing decision, not a substitute.
