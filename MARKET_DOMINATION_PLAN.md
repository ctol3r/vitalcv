# MARKET DOMINATION PLAN (GTM V2)

This plan executes the explicit mandate to turn VitalCV into a self-propagating market acquisition loop. This is an operational business wave, not a technical feature wave.

## PART 1: ACQUISITION (Outbound & Inbound)
1. **Target List Engine:** Document the `TargetList` for Staffing Firms, Credentialing Teams, and Locums Agencies (Company, Contact, Role, Status).
2. **Outbound System:** Formalize the `OutreachEngine` (Email, LinkedIn, Referrals) and track sent, replied, booked, converted.
3. **Content Engine:** Scaffold the SEO/Inbound content outlines ("How credentialing works", "Why readiness matters", "Real examples").

## PART 2: CONVERSION (Demo & Pilot)
1. **Demo Execution System:** Refine the `DemoFlow` script: NPI → decision → blockers → review (Problem → Solution → Proof in <2 minutes).
2. **First Value Metric:** Define and measure `TimeToValue` (NPI Input → Decision Shown). Goal: < 60 seconds.
3. **Pilot Conversion Loop:** Track Pilot Start → Usage → Completion. Map the explicit trigger points to convert pilots into paid seats.

## PART 3: EXPANSION & NETWORK (Retention & Referral)
1. **Expansion Engine:** Within a landed employer account, track the number of users and NPIs processed to automatically trigger "Invite Teammates" and expand seats.
2. **Referral Engine:** Document the reward structure (access/upgrades) for Employer → Employer referrals and Clinician → Employer introductions.
3. **Proof System:** Systematically capture real decisions, real outcomes, and time saved to build the sales/onboarding case studies.

## PART 4: SALES OPERATIONS
1. **Sales Pipeline Tracking:** Scaffold the tracking for Leads, Calls, Conversions, and Revenue.
2. **Validation Goals:** Secure the first 5 customers (1 → 3 → 5 employers) emphasizing speed over perfection.
3. **Loop Validation:** Ensure the full flywheel (Acquire → Demo → Pilot → Convert → Expand → Refer) repeats without friction.

---
*Execution will occur by formalizing these operational loops in the repository's documentation and tracking scaffolds.*
