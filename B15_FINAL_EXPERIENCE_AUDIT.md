# B15 Final Experience Audit

VitalCV now reads as one coherent institutional identity product.

The architecture is correct. The remaining work is almost entirely editorial and compositional.

## 1. What still feels procedural

- Some supporting labels still explain the path instead of simply moving the user through it.
- Passport still shows evidence sections that resemble an evidence bundle, even when the top decision is clear.
- Onboarding still contains a few steps of narrative framing before the continuation feels fully implicit.

## 2. What still weakens onboarding momentum

- Any extra sentence between homepage and passport slightly slows the handoff.
- Loading states still have to explain themselves, which makes them feel more like a checkpoint than a continuation.
- The product still benefits from a single shared voice with fewer fallback-style clarifications.

## 3. What still feels like enterprise tooling

- The passport still has supporting sections beneath the decision layer.
- Runtime-health remains an operator surface, even when it is short and calm.
- The product still includes a few card-based support blocks that feel more administrative than emotional.

## 4. What still weakens emotional trust

- Words like waiting, blocked, or stale are necessary, but they carry more weight when they appear more than once.
- The more the product narrates its own checking, the less inevitable it feels.
- Support language can make the surface feel careful instead of assured.

## 5. What still visually fragments the experience

- Homepage, passport, and onboarding still use slightly different support patterns.
- Badge rows and detail rows still compete occasionally with the main decision line.
- Transition states still have a different visual rhythm than the calm steady-state surfaces.

## 6. What still creates cognitive overload

- The passport can still present more than the first read needs.
- Support sections beneath the main read still ask the user to scan rather than simply act.
- Loading screens still contain a small amount of explanatory motion.

## 7. Strongest remaining conversion opportunities

- Keep cutting homepage support copy until the promise is immediate.
- Keep the passport top layer decisive and short.
- Keep onboarding as continuation, not a setup path.
- Keep deployment truth short enough to read in one glance.

## 8. Highest-leverage remaining simplifications

- Remove duplicate support sentences wherever the UI already shows the state.
- Keep a single vocabulary for identity, trust, readiness, waiting, and next step.
- Keep the next action visible before any supporting context.
- Keep deployment language operational and brief.

## Coherence View

- The product is now believable as an institutional activation platform.
- The remaining risk is not structural; it is letting the copy grow talkative again.
- The best work from here is compression, not expansion.
