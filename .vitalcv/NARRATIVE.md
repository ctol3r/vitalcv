## The Story Nobody Tells

Everyone in healthcare knows physicians are scarce. The conversation is always about
producing more — more medical schools, more residency slots, more international recruiting.

**But the bottleneck isn't production. It's throughput.**

A doctor finishes her fellowship. She accepts an offer in January.
She doesn't see a patient until May or June.
Not because she wasn't ready.
Because the hospital spent 90 days re-verifying what a state medical board
already knew, what ABIM already knew, what the DEA already knew.

Every organization in healthcare re-verifies the same clinician from scratch.
Every time. No memory. No portability.
A credential that took 12 years of education and training to earn
is treated like it might have changed since Tuesday.

---

## The Moneyball Proof

Christopher Toler ran this experiment as a clinician sourcing recruiter at Sutter Health.
Nine medical groups. Forty-five-plus specialties.
Thousands of leads — documented in the ATS.
Pipelines full. Strong interviewers.

The data was unambiguous:
- Talent was not the constraint.
- Recruiters were not the constraint.
- Interviews were not the constraint.

The drop-off was at the bottom of the funnel — after the hire, before the start.
**Credentialing. Every time.**

The Oakland A's didn't buy players. They bought runs.
*"As long as they get on first base."*

In healthcare: don't buy clinicians — **buy starts per year.**
To get more starts per year: push clinicians through credentialing faster.
VitalCV is the mechanism.

## The Job Board Discovery

Christopher also ran job posting strategy for 9 Sutter Health medical groups.
$5,000–$6,000 for a single ASCO oncology posting. 90 days.
No cap on spend. 45+ specialties. Each needing targeted specialty boards.

He eventually built the solution himself:
bought vendor packages running specialty medical association job boards,
set up automatic scraping from the home board to all specialty networks.

The insight: **the job board market is saturated and predatory.**
Clinicians rarely know what opportunities exist.
Employers pay a fortune to reach an audience that should be free to access.

VitalCV makes this free — and makes it intelligent.
Every clinician on VitalCV can see every relevant opportunity,
matched to their verified credentials, without a recruiter in the middle.

## The Non-Obvious Insight

Nobody has connected credentialing speed to workforce capacity.

Health systems measure vacancy rates. They measure time-to-fill.
Nobody measures **starts enabled per quarter** —
how many of their hires actually cleared credentialing and reached Day 1.

VitalCV introduces this metric. And then moves it.

Sell a health system on a 40% improvement in starts-per-quarter
and you've sold them on solving the 2032 provider shortage —
not with more recruiting, but with more throughput.

That is the enterprise wedge. That is the category-defining insight.
