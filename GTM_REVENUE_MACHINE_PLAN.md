# GTM + REVENUE MACHINE PLAN

This plan defines the Go-To-Market execution for VitalCV. This wave involves NO NEW PRODUCT FEATURES. It is strictly about operationalizing the sales motions, defining the Ideal Customer Profile (ICP), building the outbound/inbound engines, and tracking the core revenue metrics.

## PART 1: STRATEGIC DEFINITION (The Playbooks)
1. **ICP Definition:** Document the Primary ICP (healthcare staffing firms, credentialing teams, locums/travel agencies) including their size, workflows, pain points, and buying triggers.
2. **Sales Playbook:** Create the 10-second opener, 30-second elevator pitch, 2-minute demo script, and objection handling guides centered around "We eliminate wasted credentialing work BEFORE it starts."
3. **Live Demo Script:** Formalize the strict 2-minute demo flow: NPI → Readiness → Blockers → Decision → Employer Review.

## PART 2: CONVERSION & PRICING MECHANICS
1. **Pilot Conversion System:** Define the exact `Pilot -> Paid` triggers based on NPIs processed, decisions made, and employer engagement metrics. Add the necessary upgrade prompts.
2. **Pricing Model Implementation:** Formalize the pricing tiers: Pilot (free), Basic (usage-based per decision/provider), and Enterprise (custom).
3. **Referral Engine:** Enable network-effect sharing so clinicians sharing passports act as lead generation for employers, and employers can easily share the tool internally.

## PART 3: ACQUISITION ENGINES
1. **Outbound Engine:** Generate the target list structure (50 staffing firms, 50 recruiters) and the cadence for email/LinkedIn outreach and follow-ups.
2. **Inbound Engine:** Build the architectural scaffolding for landing pages, specific use-case pages, and SEO content.

## PART 4: CUSTOMER SUCCESS & METRICS
1. **Customer Success Loop:** For each employer, track their usage, blockers, and feedback to ensure successful onboarding calls, follow-ups, and retention.
2. **Metric System:** Scaffold the tracking for MRR, Conversion Rate, Retention, CAC, and LTV.
3. **Validation Goals:** Secure the first paying deal ($500 minimum) and validate repeatability (1 → 3 → 5 customers).

---
*Since this wave is primarily strategic and non-technical, the execution will focus on building out the documentation, pricing configurations, and metric tracking scaffolding in the repository.*
