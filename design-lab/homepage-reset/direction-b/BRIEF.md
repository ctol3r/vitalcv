## Palette (dark, warm graphite — NOT blue-black, NOT AI-gradient)
- Ground: warm graphite `#141517` (hint of warmth, zero blue cast); panel `#1C1D20`;
  raised panel `#222326`; hairline `#2E2F33`.
- Ink on dark: warm off-white `#F2F1ED`; secondary `#9C9D99`.
- Accent: work-green `#4ADE97` used ONLY for completed/moving work marks and the primary CTA
  (CTA can be solid `#2E9E6B` with off-white text for contrast). Needs-you: warm amber `#E4B45C`.
  Waiting: neutral `#6E7073`. Nothing glows. No gradients, no blur halos.
- Light sections allowed below the fold (off-white `#F2F1ED` band for employer doorway) so the
  page breathes — eyebrow inverts over it.

## Type

## Hero product surface — THE core of this direction
Autoplays once on load (~14-18s, generous pacing), replayable, scroll-scrub optional but must not
hijack. The 5 beats from the master brief rendered as a live abstract interface:
1. A masked NPI seed field types itself with placeholder glyphs (never 10 real digits, use
   "• • • •" blocks), submit pulse.
2. Fact rows assemble: labeled skeleton bars with source chips (NPPES · State board record ·
   Practice info) sliding into a profile column. Small honest captions ("We show where every fact
  came from").
3. The surface reorganizes: "Here's what still matters" — 4-6 action rows appear, each tagged with
   an owner chip: VitalCV / Your approval / You / Employer.
4. Work executes: VitalCV-owned rows tick to "Done by VitalCV" with mono timestamps; one row pauses
   at "Ready for your approval" with an approve control that self-confirms after a beat (clearly
   illustrative); one stays "Needs you"; one "Waiting on employer".
5. Resolution: a role panel slides in ("A role that fits"), application state "Applied with
   VitalCV", then a first-day progress track moves most of the way. Ends with momentum.
Static reduced-motion state: the final composed frame with all beats annotated (numbered 1-5).
