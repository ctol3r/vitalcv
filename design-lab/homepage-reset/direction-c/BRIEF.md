# DIRECTION C — PRECISION / EDITORIAL

Question: What if VitalCV had the visual confidence of a category-defining technology company
rather than healthcare software? Most visually assertive direction. Still leads with utility.
Do NOT resurrect "film" as the conceptual model.

## Palette (architectural, gallery-bright)
- Ground: gallery white `#FBFAF7`; secondary `#F3F2EE`. Ink: true black `#0E0E0C`.
- Architectural rules: black 1px lines structure the entire page (full-bleed horizontals, column
  verticals) — the layout looks drafted.
- ONE accent: signal vermilion `#D8451D` — used ONLY architecturally: index numbers, active-state
  marks, the single primary CTA, rule highlights. Small doses. Not decorative.
- State tones for the ownership model: black-on-white with vermilion marks for "needs you",
  solid-black chips for "Done by VitalCV". Muted `#8B8A85` for waiting.
- No gradients, no shadows. Flat, printed confidence.

## Type — the loudest element of this direction
- Google Fonts: "Archivo" variable (use weights 500-700 and the SemiExpanded/Expanded width axis
  for display) + "IBM Plex Mono" (400/500) for annotations.
- Display: h1 at 88-110px desktop (2 lines max), width-expanded, -0.03em, uppercase or sentence —
  pick what reads powerful and clean; mobile 44-52px. Section headers 40-56px.
- Annotations: 11px mono uppercase; index numbers (01, 02…) in vermilion mono.
- Body kept tight and small (15-16px) in narrow measures — editorial contrast between huge display
  and compact text.

## Layout grammar
- Drafted grid: visible column rules; wide editorial compositions; asymmetric placements; generous
  structural whitespace. Numbered sections 01-06 running down a fixed margin rail.
- No cards. Content sits in ruled regions.

## Eyebrow (per master spec)
- 60px, gallery-white ground, full-width 1px black rule below, 1px black rule ABOVE (the bar is a
  drafted band between two rules — distinct from A and B). Left: "VITALCV" wordmark — Archivo 650,
  15px, +0.14em tracked uppercase, small. Middle: nothing but a centered index annotation in mono
  ("N° 01 — CLINICIAN HIRING, HANDLED") that updates per section scrolled (contextual product
  state). Right: "Sign in" (11px mono uppercase) + solid black square-corner CTA "START WITH YOUR
  NPI" (42px) with vermilion hover.
- On scroll over the black outcome band: full inversion (black ground, white rules/type) with
  identical geometry. Mobile: rules hold; wordmark + black CTA "START" + mono "MENU" control
  opening a full-page black index menu with huge Archivo items.

## Hero
- Full-width drafted composition: giant display headline "Enter your NPI. / VitalCV does the rest."
  spanning the page between rules; mono annotation above it ("FOR CLINICIANS — PHYSICIANS, NPs,
  PAs, RNs"); supporting sentence in a narrow measure right-aligned or offset. The real NPI entry
  as a drafted instrument: a wide ruled field with mono placeholder + black submit block.
- Subtle employer entry: mono annotation bottom-right of hero: "HIRING? → FOR EMPLOYERS".

## How-it-works explainer — horizontal editorial sequence
- Section "02 — HOW IT WORKS": five ruled frames in a horizontal band (desktop) / vertical stack
  (mobile), each an abstract drafted illustration of one beat: masked seed → labeled fact bars with
  source annotations → remaining actions with owner marks → work resolving through states (one
  approval pause) → role + first-day meter. Frames activate in sequence as the band enters view
  (rules extend, contents draw in, ~400ms each, once). Vermilion marks show the active frame.
  Mono captions under each frame carry the story. "REPLAY" mono control.
- Reduced motion: all five frames fully drawn with captions — reads as a printed spread.

## Below
- "03 — WHAT VITALCV HANDLES": four ruled columns (VitalCV handles / Needs your approval / Needs
  you / Employer decides) with example items as typeset rows.
- "04 — THE OUTCOME": full-bleed BLACK band (eyebrow inverts): "All of it exists so you start
  sooner." + drafted role→offer→first-day track in white/vermilion.
- "05 — FOR EMPLOYERS": back to white; one strong editorial sentence + quiet CTA → /employers.
- "06 — START": repeat NPI instrument at scale. Footer: single rule, mono micro-links.

## Feeling checklist
Drafted, printed, certain. A category-defining company's public statement, in service of a simple
promise. Utility first — the reader always knows what to DO.
