# UX-01 Decision Memo — what each direction commits the whole product to

**Date:** 2026-08-08 · **For:** founder verdict · **Verdict lands in:** `design-lab/homepage-reset/DECISION.md`
**Rule:** the winner does not style one page — it becomes the global visual operating system (EC-20). Every value below back-fills the Experience Constitution the same day the verdict lands.

All three directions passed critique pass 1 on all seven tests (five-second / easy-button / distinctiveness / trust / product / employer / eyebrow). The choice is not "which page is good" — it is *which company VitalCV looks like for the next two years.*

---

## A — Operational Calm commits us to…

- **Light, bone-ground product everywhere** (`#F1F0EC` + white panels): workspace, employer, evidence surfaces all inherit the calm-console grammar. Nearest to the existing paper+ink instincts — the cheapest migration of the three.
- **Instrument Sans + IBM Plex Mono** as the product's voice: humane, quiet, never loud.
- **Spruce `#175E4C` as both the brand accent and the work/done color.** One green, one meaning — but it means the *interaction accent and the work color are the same thing*. (Creative Direction's separate indigo interaction accent retires.)
- Evidence artifacts print beautifully by default (light ground). Dark exists only if we later define an ops theme.
- **Risk:** distinctiveness is "partial by design." Calmest ≠ most memorable; next to C it will read quiet. Its thesis is that the *work* is the memorable thing.

## B — Intelligent Product commits us to…

- **A dark-first brand** (warm graphite `#141517`): the public face of VitalCV is a working instrument on dark ground. This overturns standing doctrine ("light is the only public mode"; wave-1505 LINT-04 bans dark on public routes; mainline CD's 2026-08-02 amendment allows at most *one* full-bleed Ink chapter per public page — B goes far beyond it). The verdict must explicitly rewrite those rules and define **where light bands are required** (employer doorway today; what else?).
- **Geist + Geist Mono**: precision product-grade type.
- **Work-green as the single work color and CTA**; amber = needs-you. Same accent-merges-with-work-color decision as A.
- **The agent as visible protagonist**: the hero's living work surface is not a marketing device — it is the grammar UX-07/UX-08 productize (work ledger, approval pauses, mono timestamps). Choosing B is choosing that the *product demonstration* is the brand.
- **Evidence artifacts need a defined light/print treatment** — a receipt an employer forwards must read printable; dark-ground artifacts don't. This is a real design task B creates.
- **Risk:** the 14–18s hero autoplay asks for patience (beat dots + ticker mitigate); dark-first is the biggest brand statement of the three.

## C — Precision / Editorial commits us to…

- **Gallery white `#FBFAF7`, true black ink, drafted 1px black rules product-wide**: every workspace table, every evidence artifact, every form sits in ruled regions. Strongest five-second test; "nothing in healthcare software looks like this."
- **Archivo (expanded widths) at 88–110px display** — a loud editorial register that must be enforced with discipline at product density (a credential table with drafted rules and expanded-Archivo headers is a genuinely new design problem).
- **Vermilion `#D8451D` used architecturally only** — index numbers, active marks, one CTA. States stay black/white/muted; **the accent never touches state** — the cleanest signal-vs-state separation of the three.
- Evidence artifacts read as printed/drafted documents — the strongest "could be printed and filed" fit.
- **Risk:** the far pole from calm. Daily-use fatigue in the workspace is the open question; "calm intelligence" would have to come entirely from behavior, not visuals.

---

## Named hybrids worth considering (a hybrid verdict must name its parts)

- **"A ground, B hero"** — bone/spruce system-wide, with B's living work surface as the hero demonstration. Keeps light-product doctrine, keeps the strongest easy-button proof. (Cost: B's surface loses its dark-instrument contrast; needs a re-render to prove it survives on bone.)
- **"A system, C eyebrow"** — operational calm with C's drafted two-rule eyebrow band and mono index annotations for character.
- **"C public, A workspace"** — editorial acquisition surfaces, calm working surfaces. (Cost: violates "one system" unless the split is written into the constitution as a deliberate two-register rule with shared tokens.)

## Standing recommendation (from the exploration session, unchanged)

**B — Intelligent Product**, because the program's thesis is "show, don't assert" and B is the only direction where the first thing a visitor experiences is *the product doing work*. Its two costs (dark-public doctrine rewrite, light evidence-artifact treatment) are real but bounded design tasks, not risks to truth. If the founder wants maximum calm instead, **A** is the safe global system and "A ground, B hero" preserves B's core proof.
