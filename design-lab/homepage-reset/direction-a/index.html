02Who does what

What VitalCV handles

Every item in a hire has exactly one owner. VitalCV takes what it can safely take — and never pretends to own the rest.

VitalCV handles

  • Requesting public records that describe your work
  • Drafting forms from facts you’ve already approved
  • Chasing status and sending reminders
  • Keeping your profile current as records change

Needs your approval

  • Sending anything on your behalf
  • Sharing your profile with an employer
  • Submitting an application

Needs you

  • Numbers only you hold — like a DEA registration
  • Signatures a board requires from you directly
  • Choosing which roles are worth your time

Employer decides

  • Interviews and offers
  • Their internal review steps
  • Your start date

Every action VitalCV takes is visible to you — nothing happens quietly.
