    Honest limits

    What we cannot see.

    Published here rather than discovered later. A product that reads sources for a living owes you the boundary of what it reads.

    ⊘Access required

    Sources we are not allowed to read

    NPDB and DEA registration are not open to a third party. You query them; we hold the receipt you release.

    ◐Snapshot

    Sources that publish in batches

    Several state boards publish dated files, not live records. Those lanes carry the file date, never the word “current”.

    ○Not checked

    Things nobody has asked for yet

    Privileges, employment and references need an issuer to attest. Until one does, the lane stays empty and says so.
