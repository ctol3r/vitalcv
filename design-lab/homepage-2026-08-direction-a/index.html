Six figures, and why each one is there

Drawn, not stock — a published page can load nothing from an external host, and inventing clinician photography or customer logos is the exact trap the last import fell into. Every value in every figure is a blank bar, because the real ones belong to the person looking at it. Each figure carries its own "illustrative" caption, which is also how the truth contract wants scenes labelled. The Roles section reuses the founder-approved copy from PR #1267 verbatim; the match figure shows the shape of a match and never a posting, an employer, or a count.
